An Apple outage on Tuesday, 17 November, disrupted iCloud, Apple Pay, the App Store and other services, with Apple blaming an internal update and saying it was not caused by a cyberattack, [Cybernews reported](https://cybernews.com/tech/apple-outage/).
 
## What broke and when
 
Downdetector showed a handful of people reporting connection issues around 11:30 a.m. ET (16:30 GMT), with reports jumping to the thousands around 12:29 p.m. ET (17:29 GMT), [Engadget reported](https://www.engadget.com/2319504/icloud-and-other-apple-services-are-down-in-an-apparent-outage/). Apple Pay drew about 2,300 reports in the US around 12:20 p.m. ET (17:20 GMT), and iCloud reports peaked at about 1,800, [according to The Next Web](https://thenextweb.com/news/apple-outage-internal-update-icloud-apple-pay). These are user reports, not confirmed numbers of affected users.
 
Users reported being signed out of iCloud and [unable to sign back in](https://9to5mac.com/2026/11/17/icloud-app-store-and-other-apple-services-hit-by-ongoing-outage/), the App Store failing to load, and trouble activating devices. Developers also reported problems reaching App Store Connect, and Apple retail stores were [reportedly affected](https://9to5mac.com/2026/11/17/icloud-app-store-and-other-apple-services-hit-by-ongoing-outage/), 9to5Mac said.
 
At 2:34 p.m. ET (19:34 GMT), Apple's System Status page listed iCloud sign-in as ["slow or unavailable"](https://www.engadget.com/2319504/icloud-and-other-apple-services-are-down-in-an-apparent-outage/), AppleCare on device as temporarily unavailable, and the App Store and Apple Music as down. [Business and education services](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/article/why-apple-is-down-in-a-week-of-outages-from-microsoft-to-chase-193424335.html), including Apple Business and Apple School Manager, also stayed down for a time.
 
## What Apple said
 
Apple said an internal update caused the outages and that the issue was not caused by a cyberattack, adding that many of its systems were back up and running. "We're working to get the remaining systems online as soon as possible," a spokesperson said, [Cybernews reported](https://cybernews.com/tech/apple-outage/).
 
## Is it fully fixed?
 
By the evening, Apple said all systems were working normally again, [The Next Web reported](https://thenextweb.com/news/apple-outage-internal-update-icloud-apple-pay), and its [System Status page](https://www.apple.com/support/systemstatus/) listed services as available. Apple's separate developer dashboard, however, [was still listing an App Store Connect outage](https://www.ibtimes.co.uk/apple-blames-internal-update-after-outage-hits-icloud-apple-pay-rules-out-cyberattack-1833027) after the consumer page showed recovery. Apple has not said how many users were affected.
 
The Apple outage came a day after a [bank outage hit Chase, Bank of America and Wells Fargo customers](https://signal24.info/article/bank-outage-chase-bank-of-america-and-wells-fargo-customers-hit-by-nationwide-disruption) in the US.
