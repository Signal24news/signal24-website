The Liverpool vs Man Utd match at Anfield ended 4-0 on Sunday, as Liverpool [punished Leny Yoro's early red card](https://www.espn.com/soccer/report/_/gameId/401878755) to hand Mauricio Pochettino a miserable start as Manchester United head coach.
 
United went down to 10 men in the eighth minute when Yoro fouled Alexander Isak on the edge of the area, a challenge referee Sam Barrott judged had [denied a clear goalscoring opportunity](https://www.espn.com/soccer/story/_/id/50563730/pochettino-painful-man-united-loss-liverpool). Within five minutes United were [2-0 down](https://www.nbcnewyork.com/news/sports/soccer/pochettino-man-united-debut-liverpool-premier-league/6575260/).
 
## How the goals came
 
Dominik Szoboszlai scored from the free kick that followed the red card, driving the ball [through United's wall](https://www.espn.com/soccer/report/_/gameId/401878755) in the 10th minute. Florian Wirtz made it 2-0 in the 13th minute, finishing a cross from Rio Ngumoha, according to [ESPN](https://www.espn.com/soccer/report/_/gameId/401878755) and [FotMob](https://www.fotmob.com/matches/liverpool-vs-manchester-united/2ygkcb).
 
Ngumoha scored the third himself in the 66th minute after a pass from Bradley Barcola, and substitute Victor Munoz added the fourth in the 82nd minute from a Wirtz assist, [FotMob's match data](https://www.fotmob.com/matches/liverpool-vs-manchester-united/2ygkcb) shows. The [KickOff match report](https://www.kickoff.com/world-news/english-premier-league/premier-league-match-report-liverpool-v-manchester-united-22-november-2026) lists the same four scorers.
 
## Where the result leaves both clubs
 
The defeat leaves United 19th in the table with [8 points from 11 games](https://www.espn.com/soccer/report/_/gameId/401878755), and the club has now gone [nine straight Premier League games without a win](https://sports.yahoo.com/articles/difficult-accept-mauricio-pochettino-lashes-195300886.html). Andoni Iraola's Liverpool [moved up to fourth](https://www.espn.com/soccer/report/_/gameId/401878755) with 21 points.
 
The Associated Press reported that the scoreline [equalled a club record](https://www.nbcnewyork.com/news/sports/soccer/pochettino-man-united-debut-liverpool-premier-league/6575260/) for the heaviest defeat suffered by a United manager in his first game.
 
## Pochettino: "It was clearly not a red card"
 
Pochettino was appointed after United [sacked Michael Carrick](https://www.skysports.com/football/news/13610882/mauricio-pochettino-appointed-as-manchester-united-head-coach-after-michael-carricks-sacking-at-old-trafford) with the club in the relegation zone. He left his job as head coach of the United States and signed a [two-and-a-half-year deal](https://www.goal.com/en/news/mauricio-pochettino-manchester-united-manager-usmnt-liverpool/bltce123719a1128621), after Carrick had won one of his 10 league matches this season.
 
After the game, Pochettino said Yoro's dismissal was the wrong call. "I feel like that was an action that everyone who plays football can see was clearly not a red card," he said, [according to AFP](https://www.france24.com/en/live-news/20261122-pochettino-demands-response-from-painful-man-utd-debut). He also argued that United should have had a [penalty earlier in the game](https://www.espn.com/soccer/story/_/id/50563730/pochettino-painful-man-united-loss-liverpool) for a challenge by Jeremy Jacquet on Bryan Mbeumo.
 
He told his players to use the defeat. "We need to translate that anger into an energy and start to work from tomorrow for the next game," Pochettino said, [the AP reported](https://www.nbcnewyork.com/news/sports/soccer/pochettino-man-united-debut-liverpool-premier-league/6575260/).
 
It is another heavy loss for United at Anfield, three years after their [7-0 defeat there](https://www.france24.com/en/live-news/20261122-pochettino-demands-response-from-painful-man-utd-debut) in March 2023.
