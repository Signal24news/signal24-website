The Riyadh tower attack on Tuesday, 24 November, injured 11 people when a tower in the Saudi capital's financial district was hit in what Saudi Arabia's Civil Defence called a "terrorist attack" by the Houthi group, [Arab News reported](https://www.arabnews.com/saudi-arabia/11-injured-after-houthi-attack-on-tower-in-riyadhs-financial-district-3013857).
 
Those injured were two Saudi citizens, two Indian residents, three Nepalese residents and four Egyptian residents, the Civil Defence said, [according to Anadolu Agency](https://www.aa.com.tr/en/middle-east/11-injured-in-houthi-attack-on-riyadh-s-financial-district-tower-saudi-civil-defense/4136093). Its statement did not say how badly they were hurt.
 
## What Saudi Arabia says
 
The spokesman for the Saudi-led coalition in Yemen called the strike a "cowardly terrorist attack" in a post on X and said authorities were investigating at the site, [Al Jazeera reported](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/11/24/saudi-led-coalition-condemns-terrorist-attack-on-tower-in-riyadh), citing AFP. The coalition said the tower was hit by an "unspecified projectile" and did not name the building.
 
Three witnesses told AFP that the strike hit the Public Investment Fund (PIF) tower, [according to Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/11/24/saudi-led-coalition-condemns-terrorist-attack-on-tower-in-riyadh). A fourth said a vehicle with a loudspeaker warned people in the area to take cover. No warning was sent to phones, as had happened during earlier strikes on the capital.
 
## The second attack in a week
 
The strike came less than a week after the [19 November attack](https://signal24.info/article/houthi-attack-riyadh-what-happened-what-saudi-arabia-and-the-houthis-say-and-how-it-links-to-the-iran-war) that damaged the PIF tower, the headquarters of Saudi Arabia's sovereign wealth fund and the country's tallest skyscraper, [Al Jazeera said](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/11/24/saudi-led-coalition-condemns-terrorist-attack-on-tower-in-riyadh). [Anadolu](https://www.aa.com.tr/en/middle-east/11-injured-in-houthi-attack-on-riyadh-s-financial-district-tower-saudi-civil-defense/4136093) and [Al-Monitor](https://www.al-monitor.com/originals/2026/11/least-11-injured-houthi-attack-saudi-pif-tower-what-know) also described it as a repeat attack on the King Abdullah Financial District.
 
## What the Houthis say
 
The Houthis had not commented on Tuesday's strike or claimed it, [Al-Monitor reported](https://www.al-monitor.com/originals/2026/11/least-11-injured-houthi-attack-saudi-pif-tower-what-know). Later on Tuesday, Houthi military spokesman Yahya Saree wrote on X that Saudi forces had carried out 72 airstrikes on what he called "civilian sites" in Sanaa, Yemen's capital, according to the same report. Saudi Arabia has not commented on that claim.
 
## How it fits the wider war
 
The Houthis are backed by Iran, and their attacks on Saudi Arabia have grown since the [US-Iran war](https://signal24.info/article/trump-says-a-new-iran-strike-could-come-maybe-tomorrow-as-houthis-hit-saudi-arabia-and-pakistan-pushes-for-talks) began in February. The strike on Riyadh came on the same day that Iran said more tankers had been hit in the [Strait of Hormuz](https://signal24.info/article/strait-of-hormuz-attacks-reach-their-highest-level-since-the-iran-war-began-as-two-tankers-catch-fire), where attacks on shipping are at a wartime high.
