Trump Iran tensions stayed in focus on Friday, 27 November, after President Donald Trump told US troops that the United States has "victory at hand" in the Iran war, while Iran's Revolutionary Guards claimed they forced a US F-16 to turn back near Hengam Island in the Gulf.
 
## What Trump said
 
Speaking on Thanksgiving calls to service members from his Mar-a-Lago residence in Florida on Thursday, alongside First Lady Melania Trump, Trump said the US was giving Tehran "every chance to make a deal and save a lot of lives," [ANI reported](https://www.aninews.in/news/world/us/trump-says-us-has-victory-at-hand-in-iran-war-offers-tehran-chance-for-deal20261127061105). He said he was not sure Iran's leaders would take the chance.
 
"We have victory at hand," Trump said, adding: "It will happen whenever I want it to happen," [according to Gulf News](https://gulfnews.com/world/mena/middle-east-tensions-trump-signals-victory-as-iran-offers-kuwait-drone-probe-1.500762639). He also repeated that Iran would not be allowed to get a nuclear weapon, [Telangana Today reported](https://telanganatoday.com/trump-says-iran-victory-at-hand).
 
Trump played down reports that China and Russia were helping Iran. "I think that they're very little involved with Iran," he said of China, and of Russia: "I don't think they give very much," [Gulf News reported](https://gulfnews.com/world/mena/middle-east-tensions-trump-signals-victory-as-iran-offers-kuwait-drone-probe-1.500762639).
 
## Iran's F-16 claim
 
On Friday, Iran's Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps claimed its new advanced air defence system [fired warning shots at a US F-16](https://tribune.com.pk/story/2642614/trump-says-victory-at-hand-in-iran-urges-tehran-to-reach-deal) near Hengam Island in the Gulf, forcing it to pull back, The Express Tribune reported, citing Anadolu. Iran's state news agency IRNA shared footage it said showed the jet turning away.
 
The IRGC said the jet had entered the area while escorting ships that "violated maritime regulations," according to the [same report](https://tribune.com.pk/story/2642614/trump-says-victory-at-hand-in-iran-urges-tehran-to-reach-deal). The claim has not been independently verified, and the US had not commented at the time of writing.
 
## Iran on the Kuwait attack
 
Iran's Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchi again denied that Iran was behind the [drone attack in Kuwait](https://signal24.info/article/kuwait-drone-attack-kills-two-italian-technicians-as-italy-blames-iran-and-tehran-denies-it) that killed two Italian technicians, calling it a possible "precooked recipe for war," and offered to cooperate with Kuwait and Italy on an inquiry, [Gulf News reported](https://gulfnews.com/world/mena/middle-east-tensions-trump-signals-victory-as-iran-offers-kuwait-drone-probe-1.500762639).
