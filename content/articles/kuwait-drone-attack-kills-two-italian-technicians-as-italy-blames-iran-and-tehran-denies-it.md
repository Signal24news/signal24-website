The Kuwait drone attack on Thursday, 26 November, killed two Italian technicians at an air defence site, in what Italy and Kuwait say was an Iranian strike. Tehran has [denied any involvement](https://www.yahoo.com/news/articles/two-italians-killed-iranian-drone-152440938.html), Reuters reported.
 
The two men worked for Rheinmetall Italia, the Italian unit of German defence group Rheinmetall, and were at an air defence surveillance station when it was targeted by Iranian drones, Italian Foreign Minister Antonio Tajani told the Senate in Rome, [according to Reuters](https://www.yahoo.com/news/articles/two-italians-killed-iranian-drone-152440938.html). "Two other people were injured: a Pakistani national and a captain in the Kuwaiti army," Tajani said.
 
## What Kuwait says
 
Kuwait's Ministry of Defence said its armed forces had detected several hostile drones entering the country's airspace and described the incident as an attack on a "vital facility," [Gulf News reported](https://gulfnews.com/world/mena/middle-east-conflict-saudi-refinery-attack-kills-worker-iran-denies-deadly-kuwait-strike-1.500761343). It first reported that four people had been hurt by falling debris, then [confirmed that the two Italians had died](https://www.yahoo.com/news/articles/two-italians-killed-iranian-drone-152440938.html) of their injuries.
 
## Iran denies it
 
Abolfazl Shekarchi, a senior spokesperson for Iran's armed forces, said: "No military or offensive operations whatsoever have been conducted by the Armed Forces of the Islamic Republic of Iran in the region," [CNN reported](https://edition.cnn.com/2026/11/26/middleeast/italians-killed-iranian-attack-kuwait-intl). He called it a "false flag" operation meant to raise tension in the region.
 
Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchi also "categorically rejected allegations that Iran had carried out the drone attack" in a call with Kuwait, and expressed regret over the deaths, [according to TRT World](https://www.trtworld.com/article/15e5f52a1b26). Iran's embassy in Rome also [denied the claims on X](https://www.canberratimes.com.au/story/9399160/iran-denies-attack-on-kuwait-as-two-italians-killed/).
 
## Reactions
 
Italian Prime Minister Giorgia Meloni expressed her "deepest sympathy" and offered "heartfelt condolences" to the victims' families, [CNN reported](https://edition.cnn.com/2026/11/26/middleeast/italians-killed-iranian-attack-kuwait-intl).
 
European Commission President Ursula von der Leyen wrote on social media: "We must step up pressure on Iran to put an end to these unacceptable attacks against its neighbours and our partners in the Gulf," [as quoted by Gulf News](https://gulfnews.com/world/mena/middle-east-tensions-trump-signals-victory-as-iran-offers-kuwait-drone-probe-1.500762639). The UK's Middle East minister, Stephen Doughty, also condemned the attack.
 
## Part of a wider wave of strikes
 
The Washington Post described the attack as part of [a wave of recent Iranian strikes](https://www.washingtonpost.com/world/2026/11/26/two-italians-killed-kuwait-strained-iran-ramps-up-attacks/) on US-allied Gulf states, with Bahrain and Kuwait bearing the brunt in recent weeks. Earlier in November, Iran said it had [targeted US drone and missile sites in Kuwait](https://www.canberratimes.com.au/story/9399160/iran-denies-attack-on-kuwait-as-two-italians-killed/) after the US struck Qeshm Island.
