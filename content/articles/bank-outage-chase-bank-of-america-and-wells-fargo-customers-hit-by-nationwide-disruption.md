A bank outage on Monday, 16 November, left customers of Chase, Bank of America and Wells Fargo reporting trouble logging in, checking balances and moving money. Chase said a technical bug had delayed some transactions and that customers could still reach their accounts and funds, [Yahoo Finance reported](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/currencies/articles/thousands-chase-bank-america-wells-191730678.html).
 
## What happened
 
As of 1:50 p.m. Eastern Time (ET), Downdetector showed more than 4,600 problem reports for Chase, more than 1,900 for Bank of America, more than 900 for U.S. Bank and 800 for Wells Fargo, [according to Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/currencies/articles/thousands-chase-bank-america-wells-191730678.html). These are user reports, not confirmed customer numbers. Users also reported problems with the Zelle payment service, [CBS News reported](https://www.cbsnews.com/news/banks-online-service-outages-jpmorgan-chase-bank-of-america-wells-fargo/).
 
The Chase app showed the message: "Some services aren't available. We're working on them. Please try back later." Some customers also reported ATM problems, and [a Chase customer in Dallas said](https://www.wwaytv3.com/widespread-service-outages-at-major-u-s-banks-appear-to-be-over/) he could not complete a check deposit at a branch.
 
## What the banks said
 
A Chase spokesperson said "some transactions are taking longer than usual to complete" because of a technical issue, [FOX reported](https://www.livenowfox.com/news/chase-bank-america-wells-fargo-outage). Chase described it as a technical bug rather than a cybersecurity issue, [The Daily Beast reported](https://www.thedailybeast.com/chase-bank-of-america-wells-fargo-go-down-in-third-major-outage-in-us).
 
Wells Fargo's website said online banking was temporarily unavailable and that customers could still use ATMs, branches and customer service, [Yahoo Finance reported](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/currencies/articles/thousands-chase-bank-america-wells-191730678.html). Bank of America and U.S. Bank had not made a public statement by mid-afternoon ET, and no cause was given for their problems.
 
## Is your money safe?
 
Chase said customers still had access to their accounts and funds. FDIC deposit insurance, which covers up to $250,000 per depositor, per bank, per ownership category, is not affected by an outage, [Yahoo Finance noted](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/currencies/articles/thousands-chase-bank-america-wells-191730678.html).
 
The problems came days after a global Microsoft outage that disrupted Outlook and Teams, which Microsoft said was caused by a technical issue and not a cyberattack, [CBS News reported](https://www.cbsnews.com/news/banks-online-service-outages-jpmorgan-chase-bank-of-america-wells-fargo/). No bank has linked Monday's problems to that outage.
