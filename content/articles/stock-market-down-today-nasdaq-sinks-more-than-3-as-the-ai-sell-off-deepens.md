The stock market is down today as Wall Street's sell-off in artificial intelligence shares deepened on Friday, 20 November. The Nasdaq Composite fell more than 3%, the S&P 500 slid about 2% and the Dow Jones Industrial Average dipped about 0.6%, [Rolling Out reported](https://rollingout.com/2026/11/20/stock-market-spikes-and-traders-flinch/) during afternoon trading.
 
The losses pulled the S&P 500 10% below the record high it set last month, as the market headed for a sharp weekly loss, [the Associated Press reported](https://www.timesunion.com/news/world/article/asia-shares-drop-on-ai-worries-echoing-wall-22540883.php). A 10% drop from a peak is what traders call a correction.
 
## Why is the stock market down today?
 
Investors are worried about how the AI boom will be paid for, about rising borrowing costs and about a likely Federal Reserve rate hike in December, [according to Rolling Out](https://rollingout.com/2026/11/20/stock-market-spikes-and-traders-flinch/).
 
Chipmakers led the fall. Nvidia dropped for a seventh straight session, and the Nasdaq 100 sank 3.4% by midday as investors moved money into consumer staples, health insurers and Treasuries, [Benzinga reported](https://www.benzinga.com/markets/equities/26/11/63492512/stock-market-today-nasdaq-100-nvidia-seventh-day-chipmakers-smh). AMD and Micron were among the biggest losers, [The Motley Fool reported](https://www.fool.com/coverage/stock-market-today/2026/11/20/stock-market-midday-nov-20-nasdaq-plunges-nearly-3-as-amd-and-micron-lead-a-brutal-semiconductor-sell-off/).
 
The pressure on Nvidia comes after [Jensen Huang resigned as Nvidia CEO](https://signal24.info/article/jensen-huang-resigns-as-nvidia-ceo-colette-kress-named-interim-chief), with [Colette Kress named interim chief](https://signal24.info/article/who-is-colette-kress-nvidias-interim-ceo-after-jensen-huangs-resignation).
 
Shares of AI company Anthropic also fell on their second day of trading after a record debut on Thursday, [AP reported](https://www.timesunion.com/news/world/article/asia-shares-drop-on-ai-worries-echoing-wall-22540883.php). [Fortune reported](https://fortune.com/2026/11/20/ai-stocks-selloff-anthropic-record-ipo-wall-street-split-top) that Wall Street is split on whether the AI rally has peaked.
 
## Bitcoin and gold fall too
 
The sell-off spread beyond stocks. Bitcoin was trading near $62,000 earlier on Friday and was set for another weekly decline, [according to Nexo](https://nexo.com/blog/markets-today-november-20). [CHECK latest bitcoin price before publishing]
 
Wall Street's "fear gauge," the VIX, jumped as traders braced for more swings, [Rolling Out reported](https://rollingout.com/2026/11/20/stock-market-spikes-and-traders-flinch/).
 
The fall follows a rough Thursday, when the S&P 500 lost 2% for its sixth straight decline and the Dow closed below 50,000 for the first time in more than five months, [Nexo reported](https://nexo.com/blog/markets-today-november-20).
