The CMA Awards 2026 winners were led by Ella Langley, who won six awards, including entertainer of the year, at the 60th Country Music Association Awards in Nashville on Wednesday, 18 November, [Variety reported](https://variety.com/2026/music/news/cma-awards-winners-2026-complete-list-1236952628/). Langley came into the night with a leading nine nominations, and her six wins are the most by one artist in a single night at the CMAs, according to [Variety](https://variety.com/2026/music/news/cma-awards-winners-2026-complete-list-1236952628/) and [Nashville Lifestyles](https://nashvillelifestyles.com/entertainment/celebrities/2026-cma-awards-winners-ella-langley-makes-history-with-reco/).
 
She won all five televised categories she was nominated in, ending with entertainer of the year, [Billboard reported](https://www.billboard.com/music/awards/entertainer-of-the-year-cma-awards-2026-1236385397/). Besides the top prize, she won female vocalist of the year, album of the year for "Dandelion", and single, song and music video of the year for "Choosin' Texas", according to [Good Morning America](https://www.goodmorningamerica.com/culture/story/cma-awards-2026-full-winners-list-138220240). For entertainer of the year she beat host and last year's winner Lainey Wilson, Cody Johnson, Luke Combs and Chris Stapleton, [NBC News reported](https://www.nbcnews.com/pop-culture/pop-culture-news/ella-langley-sweeps-2026-cma-awards-wins-entertainer-year-rcna610164).
 
## Records for Stapleton and Brooks & Dunn
 
Chris Stapleton won male vocalist of the year for the ninth time in his career, [PBS NewsHour reported](https://www.pbs.org/newshour/arts/ella-langley-sweeps-the-2026-cma-awards-and-wins-entertainer-of-the-year). Brooks & Dunn won vocal duo of the year for the 17th time, extending their own record, and later received the Willie Nelson Lifetime Achievement Award, according to [PBS NewsHour](https://www.pbs.org/newshour/arts/ella-langley-sweeps-the-2026-cma-awards-and-wins-entertainer-of-the-year) and [Yahoo Entertainment](https://www.yahoo.com/entertainment/music/article/cma-awards-2026-winners-list-ella-langley-sweeps-6-awards-stephen-wilson-jr-is-new-artist-of-the-year-at-47-041953036.html). Taylor Swift presented the lifetime award to the duo, [Yahoo Entertainment reported](https://www.yahoo.com/entertainment/music/live/cma-awards-2026-live-updates-lainey-wilson-hosts-taylor-swift-returns-and-ella-langley-could-win-big-210112240.html).
 
## Stephen Wilson Jr. wins new artist at 47
 
Stephen Wilson Jr., a former food scientist, won new artist of the year at 47, [Yahoo Entertainment reported](https://www.yahoo.com/entertainment/music/article/cma-awards-2026-winners-list-ella-langley-sweeps-6-awards-stephen-wilson-jr-is-new-artist-of-the-year-at-47-041953036.html). The Red Clay Strays were named vocal group of the year and Charlie Worsham won musician of the year.
 
The ceremony took place at Bridgestone Arena in Nashville with Lainey Wilson returning as host. Langley, Miranda Lambert, Kacey Musgraves, Taylor Swift, Luke Combs and Wilson were among the performers. The show aired live on ABC and Disney+ and became available to stream on Hulu the next day, [Good Morning America reported](https://www.goodmorningamerica.com/culture/story/cma-awards-2026-full-winners-list-138220240).
 
## CMA Awards 2026: main winners
 
Full list from [Billboard](https://www.billboard.com/music/awards/cma-2026-winners-list-1236385046/):
 
- Entertainer of the year: Ella Langley
- Female vocalist of the year: Ella Langley
- Male vocalist of the year: Chris Stapleton
- Album of the year: "Dandelion", Ella Langley
- Single of the year: "Choosin' Texas", Ella Langley
- Song of the year: "Choosin' Texas", written by Luke Dick, Ella Langley, Miranda Lambert and Joybeth Taylor
- Music video of the year: "Choosin' Texas", Ella Langley
- Vocal group of the year: The Red Clay Strays
- Vocal duo of the year: Brooks & Dunn
- Musical event of the year: "McArthur", Hardy, Eric Church, Morgan Wallen and Tim McGraw
- New artist of the year: Stephen Wilson Jr.
- Musician of the year: Charlie Worsham (guitar)
