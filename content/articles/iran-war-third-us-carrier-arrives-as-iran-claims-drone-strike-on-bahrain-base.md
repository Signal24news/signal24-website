The Iran war has entered a tense new phase after the USS Theodore Roosevelt arrived in the Middle East, becoming the third US aircraft carrier in the region, while Iran claimed a drone strike on a US radar in Bahrain, [Al Jazeera reported](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/11/16/uss-roosevelt-arrives-in-middle-east-is-the-us-iran-war-about-to-resume).
 
## A third US carrier
 
A US official told Al Jazeera the carrier arrived on Monday, while another official said it arrived on 15 November. It joins the USS George Washington and the USS George HW Bush in the area covered by US Central Command (CENTCOM). The Pentagon and CENTCOM have not formally confirmed the arrival, [according to Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/11/16/uss-roosevelt-arrives-in-middle-east-is-the-us-iran-war-about-to-resume).
 
Last week, US President Donald Trump said further strikes on Iran were "quite unlikely" and that the military side of the war was "effectively over", but added that the US military remained "locked and loaded" if things changed. He said the economic pressure would continue, including the naval blockade, [Al Jazeera reported](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/11/16/uss-roosevelt-arrives-in-middle-east-is-the-us-iran-war-about-to-resume).
 
## Drones over Bahrain
 
Bahrain's Defence Force said its air defences stopped several Iranian "aerial attacks" on Sunday, and the Interior Ministry issued two alerts within about three hours warning of a "potential threat", [Al Jazeera reported](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/11/15/iran-says-it-targets-us-radar-in-bahrain-what-do-we-know).
 
Iran's Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps (IRGC) said it launched six drones at a US air defence radar at Riffa Air Base in Bahrain, according to the IRGC-affiliated Fars News Agency. On Friday, the IRGC had claimed it destroyed a US early-warning radar in Bahrain. US Central Command denied that, saying it shot down two IRGC drones with no injuries or damage.
 
## The Strait of Hormuz
 
The IRGC navy said two oil tankers hit sea mines on Saturday while trying to cross the Strait of Hormuz, and claimed the ships had switched off their navigation and tracking systems and used an "unauthorised" route, [according to Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/11/15/iran-says-it-targets-us-radar-in-bahrain-what-do-we-know). The IRGC says the strait remains closed. These claims have not been independently confirmed.

## Iran's message to the region
 
Speaking at a meeting of Economic Cooperation Organization foreign ministers in Islamabad on Monday, Iran's Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchi said "strengthening energy cooperation has become an urgent and strategic necessity", and that the region needs "not only an independent security framework" but also a reliable energy mechanism, [Al Jazeera reported](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/11/16/uss-roosevelt-arrives-in-middle-east-is-the-us-iran-war-about-to-resume).
 
## "Diplomacy still has a pulse"
 
Ali Vaez, deputy programme director for the Middle East and North Africa at the International Crisis Group, warned that talks were struggling. "Diplomacy still has a pulse, but it is suffocating under the weight of mutual mistrust and maximalist demands," he told [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/11/16/uss-roosevelt-arrives-in-middle-east-is-the-us-iran-war-about-to-resume).
