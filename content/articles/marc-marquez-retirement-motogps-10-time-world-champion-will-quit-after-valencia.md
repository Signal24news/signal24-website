Marc Marquez retirement became official on Friday, when the 33-year-old Ducati rider [announced on live television](https://leadership.ng/marquez-to-retire-from-motogp-after-valencia-finale/) that Sunday's Valencia Grand Prix will be his last race, five days after [winning his eighth MotoGP title in Portugal](https://finchannel.com/marc-marquez-announces-retirement-then-sets-valencia-lap-record/143257/sport/2026/11/).
 
Instead of calling a news conference, he sat in the Ducati garage as first practice went live and [held up a series of flash cards](https://leadership.ng/marquez-to-retire-from-motogp-after-valencia-finale/), in the style of a famous scene from the 2003 film Love Actually.
 
## Why now
 
Marquez said his health made the decision. He lost [most of the 2020, 2021 and 2022 seasons](https://www.espn.in/racing/story/_/id/50613084/marc-marquez-motogp-retire-goat) to a career-threatening arm injury, and a crash at about 140 mph in Indonesia last year, a week after his seventh title, [broke his shoulder and brought back his double vision](https://www.espn.in/racing/story/_/id/50613084/marc-marquez-motogp-retire-goat).
 
He said he had started to feel fear, but [not of the bike or the speed](https://www.crash.net/motogp/news/1110959/1/marc-marquez-made-motogp-retirement-decision-october-after-fear-over-injuries); it was fear of the injury. He needed [another operation on his right shoulder](https://www.crash.net/motogp/news/1110959/1/marc-marquez-made-motogp-retirement-decision-october-after-fear-over-injuries) after Le Mans this year, and he has [crashed 14 times in 2026](https://www.espn.in/racing/story/_/id/50613084/marc-marquez-motogp-retire-goat).
 
He made up his mind [after October's Japanese Grand Prix](https://www.crash.net/motogp/news/1110959/1/marc-marquez-made-motogp-retirement-decision-october-after-fear-over-injuries) and told only his brother Alex. Ducati [found out on Thursday night](https://www.crash.net/motogp/news/1110963/1/marc-marquez-kept-retirement-decision-secret-ducati-until-thursday-valencia). In June the team had announced [a new contract running to the end of 2028](https://www.the-race.com/motogp/marquez-signs-new-ducati-motogp-deal/).
 
## The record
 
Marquez won his first world title in the 125cc class in 2010. That year in Portugal he crashed on the sighting lap, started from the back of the grid and [still won the race](https://www.espn.in/racing/story/_/id/50613084/marc-marquez-motogp-retire-goat). He went on to win six MotoGP titles with Honda and two with Ducati, in 2025 and 2026.
 
His eighth premier-class crown [equals Giacomo Agostini's record](https://leadership.ng/marquez-to-retire-from-motogp-after-valencia-finale/) and puts him one ahead of Valentino Rossi. It was also a remarkable comeback: when he returned at Mugello after missing two races, he [trailed Marco Bezzecchi by 102 points](https://www.gpblog.com/en/motogp/marc-marquez-says-goodbye-to-motogp-the-timeline-of-an-absolute-great).
 
He has [80 MotoGP wins, second only to Rossi's 89](https://www.espn.in/racing/story/_/id/50613084/marc-marquez-motogp-retire-goat), and 106 Grand Prix wins across all classes, third behind Agostini and Rossi. Speed came with risk: he crashed [109 times in the six seasons](https://www.espn.in/racing/story/_/id/50613084/marc-marquez-motogp-retire-goat) between his sixth and seventh MotoGP titles.
 
He has divided fans too. His collision with Rossi at the 2015 Malaysian Grand Prix [is still debated](https://www.espn.in/racing/story/_/id/50613084/marc-marquez-motogp-retire-goat), though ESPN's analysis of his career concludes that he is the greatest MotoGP rider of all time.
 
## What Ducati does next
 
Marquez had been set to partner [Pedro Acosta in the factory team in 2027](https://www.crash.net/motogp/feature/1110995/1/burning-questions-raised-marc-marquezs-motogp-retirement), so Ducati now has a seat to fill. Team manager Davide Tardozzi said a decision will come soon, but he [would not name any candidates](https://www.crash.net/motogp/news/1111045/1/ducati-gives-first-response-marc-marquezs-motogp-retirement-news) when asked about Fermin Aldeguer and Nicolo Bulega.
 
Hours after the announcement, Marquez finished first practice [second, 0.010 seconds behind Bezzecchi](https://www.motogp.com/en/news/2026/11/27/bezzecchi-fastest-as-marc-marquez-announces-motogp-retirement-in-valencia/1268362).
