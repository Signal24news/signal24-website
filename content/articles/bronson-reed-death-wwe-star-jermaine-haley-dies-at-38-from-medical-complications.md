Bronson Reed death news was confirmed by WWE on Friday, when the company announced that the Australian wrestler, whose real name was Jermaine Haley, had [died at the age of 38](https://www.espn.com/wwe/story/_/id/50617477/wwe-star-jermaine-haley-known-ring-bronson-reed-dies-38) due to "medical complications". WWE [did not give any further details](https://sports.yahoo.com/wrestling/articles/bronson-reed-wwe-star-dead-044223761.html).
 
"Jermaine was a larger-than-life figure in WWE, but more importantly, he was a phenomenal person, a devoted family man and a beloved colleague," [WWE said in its statement](https://variety.com/2026/tv/news/bronson-reed-dead-jermaine-haley-wwe-wrestler-1236959879/). He leaves behind [his pregnant wife, Paige, and their daughter](https://www.ynetnews.com/sport/article/s1q9thp17e).
 
## What happened at Crown Jewel
 
Reed's last appearance was at WWE's Crown Jewel event in Riyadh, Saudi Arabia, earlier this month. He got involved in the World Tag Team Championship match featuring his stablemates in The Vision, Bron Breakker and Austin Theory, and [Oba Femi slammed him off the stage through tables](https://sports.yahoo.com/wrestling/articles/full-timeline-wwe-star-bronson-052816892.html). CBS Sports noted that this kind of stunt is [performed regularly across the wrestling industry](https://www.cbssports.com/wwe/news/bronson-reed-dies-38-wwe-superstar-jermaine-haley/).
 
Two weeks before his death, Bryan Alvarez of Wrestling Observer Live reported that Reed had suffered [broken ribs and a lacerated kidney](https://bleacherreport.com/articles/25538198-wwes-bronson-reed-dies-age-38-due-medical-complications-following-crown-jewel) at the event. Fightful's Sean Ross Sapp reported that the injury forced him to [stay in Saudi Arabia](https://bleacherreport.com/articles/25538198-wwes-bronson-reed-dies-age-38-due-medical-complications-following-crown-jewel) after the rest of WWE flew home.
 
According to reports collected by Yahoo Sports, he [had surgery in Saudi Arabia](https://sports.yahoo.com/wrestling/articles/full-timeline-wwe-star-bronson-052816892.html) the day after the event and returned to the United States around 18 November. WWE never officially confirmed his injuries, and it [has not said whether his death is linked to them](https://www.ynetnews.com/sport/article/s1q9thp17e).
 
## His career
 
Born in Adelaide, Reed began wrestling in 2007 and made his name in Pro Wrestling NOAH and on the independent circuit [before joining WWE in 2019](https://www.cbssports.com/wwe/news/bronson-reed-dies-38-wwe-superstar-jermaine-haley/). He left the company for about a year from 2021 and returned in 2022.
 
Across his two spells with WWE he won the [NXT North American Championship and the Andre the Giant Memorial Battle Royal](https://www.cbssports.com/wwe/news/bronson-reed-dies-38-wwe-superstar-jermaine-haley/). He tore a biceps in February and, when he returned in September, he had [lost about 45 kg](https://sports.yahoo.com/wrestling/articles/full-timeline-wwe-star-bronson-052816892.html). CBS Sports reported he was [set for a push near the top of the card](https://www.cbssports.com/wwe/news/bronson-reed-dies-38-wwe-superstar-jermaine-haley/) through a feud with Femi.
 
## Tributes
 
Friday's SmackDown had been taped on 23 November, so WWE [added a tribute before the broadcast](https://www.ringsidenews.com/wwe-opens-smackdown-tribute-bronson-reed-following-his-passing/), opening with a black-and-white photo and the words "WWE Remembers Jermaine Haley 'Bronson Reed' 1988-2026". Rival promotions [AEW, TNA and the independent shows](https://www.soapcentral.com/entertainment/news-wwe-mourns-sudden-death-bronson-reed-38-pays-tribute-family) where he started also paid tribute, along with his friend and former WWE star [Sheamus](https://www.sportskeeda.com/wwe/news-sheamus-pays-tribute-bronson-reed-following-his-sudden-passing).
 
WWE also faced criticism from fans for using [an AI voiceover in an Instagram tribute post](https://www.givemesport.com/wwe-post-bronson-reed-death-causes-stir-accused-ai/) on Saturday.
 
## Was the WWE announcement posted three days early?
 
After the news broke, screenshots spread online showing WWE's announcement listed in Google search results as published ["3 days ago"](https://www.ringsidenews.com/wwes-bronson-reed-death-announcement-google-timestamp-explained/), which led some fans to ask whether the page had been prepared before his death.
 
Ringside News checked the page's source code and found [no publication or modification timestamp](https://www.ringsidenews.com/wwes-bronson-reed-death-announcement-google-timestamp-explained/) at all. It said WWE's website system can keep the date a page entry was first created rather than the date it went live, and that Google itself admits the dates it shows are not always accurate. The outlet said its sources confirmed that [Reed died on 27 November](https://www.ringsidenews.com/wwes-bronson-reed-death-announcement-google-timestamp-explained/), not three days earlier, and stressed that a Google timestamp is not an official publishing record. WWE has not commented on the confusion.
