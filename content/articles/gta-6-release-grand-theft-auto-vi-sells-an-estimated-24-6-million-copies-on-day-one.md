The GTA 6 release on Thursday, 19 November, brought in an estimated $2.36 billion and about 24.6 million copies sold in its first day, according to market tracker Sensor Tower, [The Statesman reported](https://www.thestatesman.com/entertainment/biggest-entertainment-launch-ever-gta-6-pulls-in-over-2-billion-on-day-one-industry-estimate-says-1503664665.html). Rockstar Games and its parent company, Take-Two Interactive, have not confirmed any sales figures.
 
Grand Theft Auto VI is the first new main game in the series since Grand Theft Auto V in 2013, a wait of 13 years and two delayed launches, [CBS News reported](https://www.cbsnews.com/news/grand-theft-auto-6-release-date-price-what-to-know/).
 
## How big was the GTA 6 launch?
 
GTA V sold 11.21 million copies and made $815.7 million in its first 24 hours in September 2013, according to Guinness World Records, [Men's Journal reported](https://www.mensjournal.com/news/gta-6-sales-19-million-copies-gta-v-record). Sensor Tower's estimate for GTA VI is nearly three times that first-day revenue, [The Statesman said](https://www.thestatesman.com/entertainment/biggest-entertainment-launch-ever-gta-6-pulls-in-over-2-billion-on-day-one-industry-estimate-says-1503664665.html), though the tracker's own figure moved by about $520 million between morning and evening on launch day.
 
Before release, Sensor Tower counted nearly 16 million pre-orders worth $1.5 billion and called it the "most pre-ordered game in history," [according to CBS News](https://www.cbsnews.com/news/grand-theft-auto-6-release-date-price-what-to-know/). Investment firm Raymond James expects Rockstar to sell 50 million copies from launch through the end of Take-Two's fiscal year on 31 March 2027.
 
## What critics say
 
GTA 6 has a score of 99 on Metacritic, tied with The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time (1998) for the highest score the site has recorded, [Forbes reported](https://www.forbes.com/sites/paultassi/2026/11/18/the-gta-6-metacritic-review-score-is-in-and-ties-an-all-time-record/). It had no mixed or negative critic reviews from more than 140 scores at the time of writing, according to [Dot Esports](https://dotesports.com/gta/news/gta-6-review-roundup), with critics praising its open world and the story of its two leads.
 
## Price, platforms and setting
 
The game is available only on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X and S. The standard edition costs $79.99 and the ultimate edition $99.99, and pre-downloads may need up to 212 GB of storage, [CBS News reported](https://www.cbsnews.com/news/grand-theft-auto-6-release-date-price-what-to-know/). Rockstar has not announced a PC version. In the past, PC versions arrived 12 to 18 months after console launches.
 
GTA VI is set in Vice City, inspired by Miami, in the fictional state of Leonida. Its leads are Jason Duval and Lucia Caminos, the series' first female protagonist, according to [CBS News](https://www.cbsnews.com/news/grand-theft-auto-6-release-date-price-what-to-know/).
 
## How much did GTA 6 cost to make?
 
Rockstar has not disclosed the budget. Analysts estimate it at more than $1 billion, and Take-Two chief executive Strauss Zelnick said only: "It was expensive, and it took a long time," [CBS News reported](https://www.cbsnews.com/news/grand-theft-auto-6-release-date-price-what-to-know/). Take-Two has also not confirmed when the new version of GTA Online will be available.
