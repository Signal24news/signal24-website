El Hierro, the smallest of Spain's main Canary Islands, has declared a social emergency after a sharp rise in migrant arrivals in the first days of November.
 
More than 3,300 people have reached the island in about 30 boats since the start of the month, [Al Jazeera reported](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/11/8/canary-islands-el-hierro-declares-emergency-amid-migrant-refugee-influx). The island council says more than 8,000 migrants have arrived over the past five weeks, [according to Euronews](https://www.euronews.com/2026/11/08/canary-islands-demand-urgent-government-action-amid-surge-in-migrant-arrivals).
 
Across all the Canary Islands, 49 boats carrying 4,587 people arrived between 1 and 6 November, according to the regional government.
 
## "Unsustainable"
 
El Hierro has about 11,400 residents and is one of Europe's main entry points for migrants crossing from West Africa. Regional leaders have called the surge [unsustainable](https://www.reuters.com/world/migrant-surge-spains-canary-islands-is-unsustainable-authorities-say-2026-11-06/), Reuters reported.
 
Alpidio Armas, who heads El Hierro's governing council, raised concern over the "sharp increase in arrivals". Officials are preparing temporary accommodation, and Armas said conditions at the island's hospital were "undesirable" as pressure on its resources grows.
 
## Madrid steps in
 
Canary Islands president Fernando Clavijo said he [briefed Prime Minister Pedro Sánchez by phone](https://x.com/FClavijoBatlle/status/2119109486057038028) on the situation and on growing discontent among residents.
 
Sánchez agreed to call the Interministerial Commission on Immigration at the Moncloa Palace in Madrid on Tuesday, 10 November, and to strengthen healthcare on the island, [Infobae reported](https://www.infobae.com/espana/2026/11/07/pedro-sanchez-se-compromete-a-reforzar-la-sanidad-en-el-hierro-y-convocar-la-comision-interministerial-de-inmigracion/). The meeting will be chaired by Territorial Policy Minister Ángel Víctor Torres, with Clavijo and Armas taking part.
 
## Why now
 
Clavijo said calm sea conditions had contributed to the surge, according to Reuters.
 
The increase follows a much quieter first half of the year. The UN refugee agency, UNHCR, [recorded 3,708 sea arrivals](https://data.unhcr.org/fr/documents/download/123495) in the Canary Islands between January and June 2026.
