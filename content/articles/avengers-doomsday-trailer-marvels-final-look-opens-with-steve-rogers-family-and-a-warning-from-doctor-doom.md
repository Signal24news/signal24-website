The final [Avengers Doomsday trailer](https://www.goodmorningamerica.com/culture/story/avengers-doomsday-final-trailer-released-138375955) arrived on Monday, 23 November, less than a month before the film opens. It begins with Steve Rogers and Peggy Carter at home before the Fantastic Four arrive, and [Robert Downey Jr.'s Doctor Doom](https://www.yahoo.com/entertainment/movies/trailers/articles/avengers-doomsday-final-trailer-reveals-161500670.html) is heard warning that everyone has lived "stolen lives".
 
## What the Avengers Doomsday trailer shows
 
The clip runs about three minutes and [picks up after a scene added to the Avengers: Endgame re-release](https://variety.com/2026/film/news/avengers-doomsday-trailer-captain-america-son-cyclops-x-men-1236893948/) that revealed the couple's teenage son. It opens with Chris Evans' Steve and Hayley Atwell's Peggy discussing whether to tell the boy that Steve is Captain America. A flash of light then brings Reed Richards, Sue Storm, Ben Grimm and Johnny Storm into their front yard, [ABC News reported](https://abcnews.com/GMA/Culture/avengers-doomsday-final-trailer-released/story?id=138375955).
 
Steve [tells the Fantastic Four](https://gizmodo.com/everything-we-noticed-in-the-final-trailer-for-avengers-doomsday-2000843986) that the Avengers did what was needed years ago but did not know their choices would have consequences. Doom's "stolen lives" warning plays over footage of the villain [alongside three Latverian witches](https://www.yahoo.com/entertainment/movies/trailers/articles/avengers-doomsday-final-trailer-teases-154858567.html), and Thor confronts him.
 
Steve then leaves his family to go with the Fantastic Four and is seen calling Mjolnir to his hand. The footage also brings in Shang-Chi, the New Avengers and the X-Men. [James Marsden's Cyclops](https://www.yahoo.com/entertainment/movies/trailers/articles/final-avengers-doomsday-trailer-reveals-175737859.html) calls out "To me, my X-Men", and Nightcrawler [teleports Gambit, Mystique and Beast](https://www.yahoo.com/entertainment/movies/article/avengers-doomsday-final-trailer-gives-first-look-at-hailee-steinfeld-plus-3-other-things-we-spotted-144448737.html) into the fight. Marvel released its first full trailer [on 20 July](https://abcnews.com/GMA/Culture/avengers-doomsday-trailer-release-date-marvel-movie/story?id=134860279).
 
## Who is confirmed in the cast
 
Hailee Steinfeld joined the cast on Sunday, 22 November, when [Marvel Studios and Disney Animation announced her return as Kate Bishop](https://variety.com/2026/film/news/hailee-steinfeld-avengers-doomsday-kate-bishop-1236955585/) in an Instagram post promoting her animated film Hexed. She appears in the final trailer.
 
The cast also includes Chris Hemsworth's Thor and returning X-Men actors including Patrick Stewart, whose [death in October](https://signal24.info/article/patrick-stewart-cause-of-death-death-certificate-lists-lewy-body-disorder-and-cardiopulmonary-arrest) means Doomsday will be one of his last film roles. Anthony and Joe Russo return to direct, and Marvel says the film puts the Avengers, X-Men and Fantastic Four [on screen together for the first time](https://www.marvel.com/articles/movies/avengers-doomsday-final-trailer) in its cinematic history.
 
## Release date and what comes next
 
Avengers: Doomsday opens in cinemas on Friday, 18 December, followed by [Avengers: Secret Wars on 17 December 2027](https://www.marvel.com/movies/avengers-doomsday).
