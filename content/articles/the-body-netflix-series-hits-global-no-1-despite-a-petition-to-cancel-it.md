The Body Netflix series, Quinn Shephard's eight-part horror thriller set at a Catholic school in 2004, is the most-watched English-language show on Netflix worldwide. It topped the chart for the week of 16 to 22 November, even as a petition from religious campaigners calls for it to be pulled.
 
## What it is
 
The show follows the dance team at St. Mark's Catholic School, whose [Halloween initiation ritual goes badly wrong](https://movieweb.com/the-body-streaming-hit-netflix-november-2026/). Strange visions and religious paranoia then spread through their community. It arrived on 11 November and is rated TV-MA.
 
Shephard created the series and directed the first episode. She also made the Emmy-nominated mystery Under the Bridge, and she calls this her ["most personal" story](https://www.netflix.com/tudum/articles/the-body-plot-release-date-news). Gina Gammell is co-showrunner, and Riley Keough is among the executive producers.
 
Kristina Bogic and Sara Boustany play team co-captains Anya and Elise, and Geena Meszaros plays Lisbeth. The cast also includes [Sofia Wylie, Shirley Chen, Marcel Ruiz and Nnamdi Asomugha](https://collider.com/the-body-netflix-review/), who plays school principal Father Franklin.
 
## The numbers
 
The series debuted at No. 3 on Netflix's English TV chart with [5.1 million views and 37.4 million hours watched](https://movieweb.com/the-body-streaming-hit-netflix-november-2026/) in its first five days. In its second week, [it rose 86% to 9.5 million views](https://www.thewrap.com/creative-content/tv-shows/the-body-netflix-tv-ratings-second-week/) and took No. 1, ahead of Black Doves season 2 with 3.7 million. Netflix publishes the weekly figures [on its official Top 10 site](https://www.netflix.com/tudum/top10).
 
## The backlash
 
The conservative Christian group CitizenGO [launched its petition on 5 November](https://movieweb.com/the-body-netflix-cancellation-petition/), six days before the premiere, urging Netflix to cancel what it called "a blasphemous anti-Christian series featuring satanic ritual." The petition had about [169,000 signatures by 23 November](https://www.newsweek.com/petition-to-cancel-blasphemous-netflix-hit-the-body-surges-12757922), Newsweek reported. Netflix has not responded.
 
## Critics and audiences
 
The show debuted at 100% on Rotten Tomatoes before settling. It now sits at [84% from critics and 95% with audiences](https://www.rottentomatoes.com/tv/the_body_2026).
 
Collider's reviewer called it [moodier and more thoughtful than a typical teen thriller](https://collider.com/the-body-netflix-review/), more interested in faith, gender roles and internalised homophobia than in unmasking a culprit, and praised the young cast as its greatest asset. The same review faulted its thin and sometimes confusing plotting and very dark cinematography.
 
## A second season?
 
Netflix has not announced one. The creators say they have [a few seasons of story arcs mapped out](https://www.thewrap.com/creative-content/tv-shows/the-body-quinn-shephard-gina-gammell-season-1-finale-interview/) and hope for more than two.
