Colette Kress, Nvidia's chief financial officer since 2013, is now running the company as interim CEO after Jensen Huang's resignation, until a permanent chief executive is chosen.
 
Nvidia's board accepted Huang's resignation on 11 November and named Kress, 59, interim chief executive. She will remain chief financial officer while serving in the role, [according to the company's SEC filing](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1045810/000104581026000082/nvda-20261111.htm). Read our report on [Jensen Huang's resignation](https://signal24.info/article/jensen-huang-resigns-as-nvidia-ceo-colette-kress-named-interim-chief).
 
## Career and education
 
Kress has worked in finance roles in the technology industry for nearly three decades. She held positions at Texas Instruments, Microsoft and Cisco before joining Nvidia in September 2013, [Fortune reported](https://fortune.com/2026/11/12/colette-kress-interim-chief-exeuctive-office-nvidia-jensen-huang-never-sought-c-suite-career-climb-billionaire-finance-leader/). She has a bachelor's degree in finance from the University of Arizona and an MBA from Southern Methodist University, according to Fortune.
 
Kress had not always planned to become a senior executive. In a 2022 interview with Fortune, she said reaching the C-suite was never necessarily her career goal. "I think honestly, throughout my career, I was just always fascinated by somebody else's role," she said, [as Fortune recalled this week](https://fortune.com/2026/11/12/colette-kress-interim-chief-exeuctive-office-nvidia-jensen-huang-never-sought-c-suite-career-climb-billionaire-finance-leader/).
 
## How Nvidia changed under her watch as CFO
 
When Kress joined Nvidia, the company was known mainly for its graphics cards. It has since become a major supplier of chips for artificial intelligence. Nvidia's annual revenue rose from $4.13 billion in fiscal 2014 to $130.5 billion in fiscal 2025, [Fortune reported](https://fortune.com/2026/11/12/colette-kress-interim-chief-exeuctive-office-nvidia-jensen-huang-never-sought-c-suite-career-climb-billionaire-finance-leader/).
 
## What happens next
 
Nvidia's board also appointed Harvey C. Jones, 73, as chair of the board, and is conducting a search for a new chief executive, [the SEC filing says](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1045810/000104581026000082/nvda-20261111.htm). The company has not said when a successor will be chosen.
