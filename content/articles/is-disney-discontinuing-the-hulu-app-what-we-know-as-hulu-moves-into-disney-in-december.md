The Disney Hulu app change is coming in December, when Disney will bring Hulu and Disney+ together in one app, the company told shareholders. But Disney has [not announced a shutdown date](https://qz.com/disney-hulu-disney-plus-app-merger-december-112326) for the standalone Hulu app.
 
## What Disney said
 
In its quarterly letter to shareholders, Disney CEO Josh D'Amaro and CFO Hugh Johnston wrote that subscribers will have "a fully unified experience on Disney+, including live TV and select premium add-ons," [Quartz reported](https://qz.com/disney-hulu-disney-plus-app-merger-december-112326). The letter said the app unification was "nearly complete" and would begin in December, [according to Parade](https://parade.com/news/disney-discontinues-hulu-standalone-app).
 
Hulu's live TV service will also come to Disney+ in December, [Quartz said](https://qz.com/disney-hulu-disney-plus-app-merger-december-112326), citing Gizmodo.
 
## Is the Hulu app shutting down?
 
Not on a set date yet. Several outlets report that the standalone app is likely to go. An internal Disney document obtained by Business Insider said Hulu's app and technology will be [decommissioned after subscribers move to Disney+](https://wchstv.com/news/nation-world/is-disney-getting-rid-of-the-hulu-app-what-we-know-streaming-plus-platform-unified-experience-live-tv-espn-premium-subscriptions-cost-subscribers-movies). [Variety and Forbes](https://www.yahoo.com/entertainment/tv/articles/disney-phasing-hulu-app-still-134234349.html) also reported that Disney plans to phase out the Hulu app.
 
Disney itself has [not confirmed when that final step will happen](https://uk.news.yahoo.com/disney-discontinuing-standalone-hulu-app-021132990.html), or how long subscribers will have to make the switch.
 
## What it means for Hulu subscribers
 
For now, standalone Hulu subscriptions in the US [remain available](https://qz.com/disney-hulu-disney-plus-app-merger-december-112326), and the full Hulu catalogue is set to be carried over into Disney+. Hulu shows will still be there, but users will watch them inside the Disney+ app, [USA Today reported](https://www.yahoo.com/entertainment/tv/articles/disney-phasing-hulu-app-still-134234349.html).
 
Disney's executives said the change is meant to make it [easier for viewers to find and explore content](https://qz.com/disney-hulu-disney-plus-app-merger-december-112326) from all of the company's brands and franchises in one place.
