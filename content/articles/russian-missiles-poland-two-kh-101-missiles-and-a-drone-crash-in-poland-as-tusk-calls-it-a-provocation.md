Russian missiles hit Poland on Friday, 20 November, when two Kh-101 cruise missiles and a jet-powered drone fell on Polish territory during Russia's overnight attack on Ukraine, Defence Minister Wladyslaw Kosiniak-Kamysz told a press conference, [the Kyiv Independent reported](https://kyivindependent.com/poland-reports-airspace-violation-during-russian-attack-on-ukraine-investigates-debris-crash/).
 
The missiles came down at Nowy Lipowiec and Rudnik nad Sanem, both in southeastern Poland near the Ukrainian border, [according to the Kyiv Independent](https://kyivindependent.com/poland-reports-airspace-violation-during-russian-attack-on-ukraine-investigates-debris-crash/). The Kh-101 missile at Nowy Lipowiec fell in a rural area about 31 miles (50 km) from the border, [UPI reported](https://www.upi.com/Top_News/World-News/2026/11/20/poland-russian-missiles-hit-poland/7571795188566/), citing the BBC.
 
There were no reports of injuries or damage to critical infrastructure in Poland, Kosiniak-Kamysz said, adding that there was "no doubt" the objects were Russian, [Notes from Poland reported](https://notesfrompoland.com/2026/11/20/unidentified-object-hits-poland-amid-russian-attacks-on-western-ukraine/).
 
## What Poland and NATO said
 
Prime Minister Donald Tusk said it appeared that "we are dealing with an intentional provocation" by Russia, timed to coincide with European military exercises in Poland for a possible postwar mission in Ukraine, [the Associated Press reported](https://www.usnews.com/news/world/articles/2026-11-20/russian-attacks-on-kyiv-kill-3-cripple-bridges-and-cut-power). He said it was not likely an accident, according to [UPI](https://www.upi.com/Top_News/World-News/2026/11/20/poland-russian-missiles-hit-poland/7571795188566/).
 
NATO Secretary-General Mark Rutte called the incident a "grave violation" of Poland's airspace, [AP reported](https://www.usnews.com/news/world/articles/2026-11-20/russian-attacks-on-kyiv-kill-3-cripple-bridges-and-cut-power).
 
Late on Friday, Poland's Operational Commander, General Ireneusz Nowak, said 11 objects approached the Polish border and three of them crossed it, [according to a 20:00 CET situation report](https://www.gscentre.eu/post/russian-missiles-poland-risk-assessment-update-20-november-2026-2000).
 
Russia has not claimed responsibility for the objects that fell in Poland, [UPI reported](https://www.upi.com/Top_News/World-News/2026/11/20/poland-russian-missiles-hit-poland/7571795188566/).
 
## A huge attack on Ukraine
 
The objects crossed into Poland during what Kosiniak-Kamysz called "one of the largest Russian attacks on Ukraine since the beginning of the full-scale war," [Notes from Poland reported](https://notesfrompoland.com/2026/11/20/unidentified-object-hits-poland-amid-russian-attacks-on-western-ukraine/). The attack killed at least eight people in Ukraine, according to [UPI](https://www.upi.com/Top_News/World-News/2026/11/20/poland-russian-missiles-hit-poland/7571795188566/).
 
It is not the first time Russia's war has spilled into Poland. In July this year, [a Russian Kh-101 missile landed in a field in the Lublin region](https://kyivindependent.com/russian-kh-101-missile-fall-in-poland-during-mass-attack-on-ukraine-official-confirms/), and in November 2022, a [missile explosion in the village of Przewodow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022_missile_explosion_in_Poland) killed two people during Russian strikes on Ukraine.
