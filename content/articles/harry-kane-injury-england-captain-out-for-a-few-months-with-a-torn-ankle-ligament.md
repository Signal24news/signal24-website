The Harry Kane injury is a major blow for Bayern Munich and England. The Ballon d'Or winner tore a ligament in his left ankle in Friday's 1-1 Bundesliga draw with Cologne and [faces several months on the sidelines](https://www.espn.com/soccer/story/_/id/50543079/harry-kane-injury-bayern-munich).
 
## How it happened
 
[Kane went down](https://www.bundesliga.com/en/bundesliga/news/harry-kane-ankle-injury-bayern-munich-cologne-england-40055) at the Allianz Arena on Friday, 20 November, after a challenge from Cologne defender Joel Schmied, and was replaced by Tom Bischof in the 20th minute. Schmied reached Michael Olise's low cross first but [caught Kane's left leg](https://edition.cnn.com/2026/11/20/sport/harry-kane-ankle-injury) as he followed through at the back post. Kane got back to his feet and briefly tried to play on before [hobbling off](https://edition.cnn.com/2026/11/20/sport/harry-kane-ankle-injury).
 
Bayern were trailing 1-0 to [Mikey Moore's 12th-minute goal](https://www.espn.com/soccer/story/_/id/50543079/harry-kane-injury-bayern-munich) at the time and equalised through Nathaniel Brown before half-time.
 
## What Bayern have said
 
After an examination by its medical department, [Bayern confirmed](https://edition.cnn.com/2026/11/20/sport/harry-kane-ankle-injury) that Kane had torn the syndesmosis ligament in his left ankle and would be out "for the next few months". Sporting director Max Eberl said Bayern expect him to miss [about three months](https://www.espn.com/soccer/story/_/id/50543079/harry-kane-injury-bayern-munich). Head coach Vincent Kompany said it would be ["months rather than weeks"](https://www.bundesliga.com/en/bundesliga/news/harry-kane-ankle-injury-bayern-munich-cologne-england-40055).

## Which games he could miss
 
Kane is almost certain to [miss](https://www.espn.com/soccer/story/_/id/50543079/harry-kane-injury-bayern-munich) Bayern's remaining four Champions League league-phase games. A three-month absence would also rule him out of [11 Bundesliga games and a German Cup tie against Hertha Berlin](https://www.skysports.com/football/news/13614559/harry-kane-england-and-bayern-munich-striker-set-to-miss-three-months-with-ankle-injury). Bayern's next game is [at Lille on 25 November](https://www.bundesliga.com/en/bundesliga/news/harry-kane-ankle-injury-bayern-munich-cologne-england-40055).
 
The timing is awkward for Kompany. Michael Olise was [forced off at half-time with a bruised foot](https://www.bundesliga.com/en/bundesliga/news/harry-kane-ankle-injury-bayern-munich-cologne-england-40055), and several other players, including Luis Diaz, Serge Gnabry, Alphonso Davies and Manuel Neuer, are [already injured](https://www.bundesliga.com/en/bundesliga/news/harry-kane-ankle-injury-bayern-munich-cologne-england-40055). Kane and Olise account for [22 of Bayern's 54 goals](https://www.espn.com/soccer/story/_/id/50543079/harry-kane-injury-bayern-munich) this season.
 
Since joining from Tottenham in 2023, Kane has scored [156 goals in 161 games](https://edition.cnn.com/2026/11/20/sport/harry-kane-ankle-injury) for Bayern. Last month he became the [first Englishman in 25 years to win the Ballon d'Or](https://edition.cnn.com/2026/11/20/sport/harry-kane-ankle-injury), and he recently signed a contract that keeps him at Bayern [until June 2029](https://edition.cnn.com/2026/11/20/sport/harry-kane-ankle-injury).
 
## What it means for England
 
England's next big test is the Nations League quarter-final against Norway, with the first leg in [Oslo on 27 March](https://www.skysports.com/football/news/13614559/harry-kane-england-and-bayern-munich-striker-set-to-miss-three-months-with-ankle-injury) and the return leg at Wembley on 30 March. If his recovery goes to plan, Kane would have [just over a month to prepare](https://www.skysports.com/football/news/13614559/harry-kane-england-and-bayern-munich-striker-set-to-miss-three-months-with-ankle-injury), and the injury will [worry England head coach Thomas Tuchel](https://www.skysports.com/football/news/13614559/harry-kane-england-and-bayern-munich-striker-set-to-miss-three-months-with-ankle-injury).
 
The injury comes days after Kane became England's [record appearance-maker](https://www.bundesliga.com/en/bundesliga/news/harry-kane-breaks-england-all-time-caps-record-bayern-ballon-dor-39443) and scored in the [win over Spain](https://signal24.info/article/spain-vs-england-10-man-england-win-3-2-in-madrid-to-end-spains-43-game-unbeaten-run).
