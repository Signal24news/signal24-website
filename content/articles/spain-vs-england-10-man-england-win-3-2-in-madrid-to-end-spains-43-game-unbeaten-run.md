England beat world champions Spain 3-2 in Madrid on Sunday, ending Spain's record 43-match unbeaten run despite playing the last half hour with 10 men, [TSN reported](https://www.tsn.ca/soccer/article/england-ends-spains-43-game-unbeaten-streak-with-win-in-nations-league/).
 
The win sent England top of Nations League Group A3 on goal difference, with both sides finishing on 15 points, [The National reported](https://www.thenationalnews.com/sport/football/2026/11/16/nations-league-england-end-spains-long-unbeaten-run-with-massive-win-in-madrid/). Both teams had already qualified for the quarter-finals.
 
## How the match unfolded
 
Harry Kane put England ahead from the penalty spot just before half-time, after a foul by Pau Cubarsí was upheld on video review. It was Kane's 93rd England goal, extending his national record.
 
Ezri Konsa headed in a Declan Rice corner in the 53rd minute, and Ben White made it 3-0 soon after, set up by Jude Bellingham.
 
Spain hit back after England midfielder Elliot Anderson was sent off in the 63rd minute for bringing down Lamine Yamal. Mikel Oyarzabal scored the penalty, and Nico Williams pulled another goal back from a Yamal pass in the 75th minute, but England held on.
 
## End of a record run
 
England became the first team to beat Spain in normal or extra time since Colombia in March 2024, according to The National. Spain's run did include a penalty shoot-out defeat to Portugal.
 
The result reversed September's meeting at Wembley, when Spain won 3-2, [Al Jazeera reported](https://www.aljazeera.com/sports/2026/9/26/yamal-nets-in-spains-3-2-comeback-win-against-england-in-nations-league).
 
## What they said
 
"This was the highest level in terms of belief, team spirit, togetherness," England manager Thomas Tuchel said. He added that the red card and penalty "changed the momentum" of the game.
 
"It's a massive win," said midfielder Declan Rice.
