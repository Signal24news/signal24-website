US President Donald Trump has once again mentioned the 2025 war between Pakistan and India, claiming that 11 aircraft were shot down during it. He says he had threatened trade sanctions and heavy tariffs to prevent a war between the two nuclear powers.

Speaking at a rally in Florida, Trump claimed that he had played a role in stopping eight wars around the world, yet he did not receive the Nobel Peace Prize. According to him, five of these conflicts were helped to be prevented through tariffs.

Trump said that he had given a clear message to Pakistan and India that if the war was not stopped, heavy trade tariffs would be imposed on both countries and they would face difficulties in trading with the United States. He further claimed that the United Nations had not helped in stopping the war and that stopping all wars was a matter of honor for him.

The US President also made a strong statement regarding Iran, saying that the US could conduct another operation against Iran, but the US has the ability to end the war quickly. According to him, the real question is whether to end the war or let Iran weaken further.

Trump added that after the Iran issue, the US may also turn its attention to Cuba. He said that US Secretary of State Marco Rubio will be sent to finalize the agreement.

Talking about the situation in the Strait of Hormuz, Trump said that oil shipments are continuing through this important sea passage. He claimed that the US is obtaining oil from the Strait of Hormuz as it wishes and more oil is being exported through this route than before the war started.
