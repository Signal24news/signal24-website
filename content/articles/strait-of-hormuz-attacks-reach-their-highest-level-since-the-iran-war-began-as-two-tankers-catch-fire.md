Strait of Hormuz attacks on shipping have reached their highest level since the Iran war began in late February, maritime data shows, and two oil tankers caught fire in the strait on Monday, 23 November.
 
## Two tankers on fire
 
Iranian media reported that two oil tankers [caught fire in the Strait of Hormuz](https://www.newarab.com/news/iran-and-us-trade-threats-tankers-burn-hormuz), with Iran's navy saying the ships had entered an unauthorised area. [The National](https://www.thenationalnews.com/news/mena/2026/11/23/live-iran-us-war-trump/) also reported the Iranian navy's account. There has been no independent confirmation of what caused the fires.
 
## Attacks at a wartime high
 
More than 30 attacks on ships were reported in October, the [first month above 30](https://sg.news.yahoo.com/iranian-attacks-strait-hormuz-highest-151328519.html) since the war began, according to a CNN analysis of data from the United Kingdom Maritime Trade Operations (UKMTO). At least 22 attacks had been reported in November by Sunday, the second-highest monthly figure of the war. Before October, reported attacks in the region averaged about 10 a month.
 
UKMTO's reporting area covers the Strait of Hormuz, the Persian Gulf and the Gulf of Oman. In its [20 November overview](https://www.ukmto.org/-/media/ukmto/products/20261120-ukmto_vra_overview_u.pdf?rev=5dbbfece42834c3896b685bc2223e1aa), UKMTO said it only reports incidents it can confirm from primary sources, so the real number may be higher. Data from the International Maritime Organization also shows October with the [most confirmed incidents so far this year](https://sg.news.yahoo.com/iranian-attacks-strait-hormuz-highest-151328519.html), although figures vary between sources.
 
## Iran vows to fight the blockade
 
Iran "will use all available tools to confront the US naval blockade," Foreign Ministry spokesperson Esmaeil Baghaei told his [weekly news conference on Monday](https://www.newarab.com/news/iran-and-us-trade-threats-tankers-burn-hormuz). He said Iran would honour its commitments on the strait "as soon as possible" if the other side met its conditions. [The FDD overnight brief](https://www.fdd.org/overnight-brief/november-23-2026/) carried the same blockade warning.
 
The US has kept up its naval blockade of Iranian ports since [restoring it on 14 July](https://www.newarab.com/news/iran-and-us-trade-threats-tankers-burn-hormuz), after an earlier draft understanding with Tehran collapsed.
 
## Talks continue
 
Qatar's prime minister and foreign minister, Sheikh Mohammed bin Abdulrahman Al Thani, was due to meet US Secretary of State Marco Rubio in Washington on Monday, according to [Rubio's official schedule](https://www.newarab.com/news/iran-and-us-trade-threats-tankers-burn-hormuz), with talks expected to focus on Qatar's efforts to end the war. A [Qatari delegation](https://www.fdd.org/overnight-brief/november-23-2026/) also held talks in Tehran on Friday night, an Iranian official said.
 
Israeli media reported that Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu is still [pressing President Donald Trump to attack Iran "as soon as possible"](https://www.newarab.com/news/iran-and-us-trade-threats-tankers-burn-hormuz).
 
## Why it matters
 
About [one-fifth of the world's oil](https://sg.news.yahoo.com/iranian-attacks-strait-hormuz-highest-151328519.html) passed through the Strait of Hormuz before the war. Global oil prices are now falling, which the International Energy Agency links to a recovery in Gulf exports. Maritime data firm Kpler has said Iran's oil revenues are [falling sharply](https://sg.news.yahoo.com/iranian-attacks-strait-hormuz-highest-151328519.html) under the US blockade and could reach zero by mid-December.
