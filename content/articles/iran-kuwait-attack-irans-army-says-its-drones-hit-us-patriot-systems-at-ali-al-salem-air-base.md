In the latest Iran Kuwait attack, Iran's army said its drones targeted US Patriot air defence systems at Ali Al Salem Air Base in Kuwait early on Wednesday, 18 November, according to [a statement carried by state broadcaster IRIB and reported by Dawn](https://www.dawn.com/news/2045376/iran-army-says-targeted-us-military-equipment-at-kuwait-base-irib). The claim has not been independently confirmed.
 
## What Iran claims
 
The army statement said "combat and kamikaze drones" had targeted "Patriot air defence systems and drone and missile launch platforms" belonging to the US military at the base, [Dawn reported, citing AFP](https://www.dawn.com/news/2045376/iran-army-says-targeted-us-military-equipment-at-kuwait-base-irib). [Middle East Eye](https://www.middleeasteye.net/news/iran-launches-attacks-military-base-kuwait) carried the same statement from Iranian state media.
 
"We will not bow to any power in defending Iran," the army said, warning that its response to any threat would be "decisive, forceful and without geographical limits," [according to Tasnim news agency](https://www.tasnimnews.ir/en/news/2026/11/18/3751631/iranian-army-drones-hit-us-air-base-in-kuwait). The statement gave no evidence of damage to the systems it named.
 
## What Kuwait and the US say
 
Kuwait's military said its air defences were engaging "hostile" drones early on Wednesday, and that "any explosions that might be heard would be the result of air defence systems intercepting the attacks," [Anadolu Agency reported via The Express Tribune](https://tribune.com.pk/story/2640955/iranian-army-says-drone-strikes-targeted-us-installations-at-kuwait-air-base). It did not report damage or casualties.
 
Kuwait's armed forces had also said on Tuesday night that air defences were responding to "hostile drone attacks," without saying where the drones came from, [Al Jazeera reported](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/11/17/kuwait-reports-drone-attacks-as-iran-us-trade-attacks-on-qeshm-and-bahrain).
 
None of the reports we checked included a response from the US military to Iran's claim about the Patriot systems.
 
## Strikes on Qeshm and Bahrain
 
The drone claim came a day after the US struck Iran's Qeshm Island in the Strait of Hormuz. A US official told Al Jazeera the strike hit a missile launch site in response to Iran's earlier attack on a US base in Bahrain, [Al Jazeera reported](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/11/17/kuwait-reports-drone-attacks-as-iran-us-trade-attacks-on-qeshm-and-bahrain).
 
Iran's Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps (IRGC) had said it carried out a "precision operation" with drones on a US-operated radar at Sheikh Isa air base in Bahrain. US Central Command said the drones were "intercepted without any damage to US equipment or personnel," [according to Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/11/17/kuwait-reports-drone-attacks-as-iran-us-trade-attacks-on-qeshm-and-bahrain). AFP said it was the third Iranian drone attack on Bahrain in five days.
 
Ahmad Nafisi, deputy governor of Iran's Hormozgan province, told the IRNA news agency that the US attack on Qeshm "caused no damage to residential, commercial, or infrastructural areas." IRNA reported five to six explosions on the island on Tuesday evening, [Al Jazeera said](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/11/17/kuwait-reports-drone-attacks-as-iran-us-trade-attacks-on-qeshm-and-bahrain).
 
Read our earlier report on [Iran's claimed drone strike on a US base in Bahrain](https://signal24.info/article/iran-war-third-us-carrier-arrives-as-iran-claims-drone-strike-on-bahrain-base).
 
## Tanker attacks in the Strait of Hormuz
 
Attacks on shipping have also returned. The Panama-flagged tanker Tara Gas was struck on Monday about seven nautical miles north of Khasab, Oman, causing a fire in its engine room, according to the security firm Vanguard, [Al Jazeera reported](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/11/17/kuwait-reports-drone-attacks-as-iran-us-trade-attacks-on-qeshm-and-bahrain). Vanguard and India's ports minister first said all 25 crew, 24 of them Indian, were safe. The UK Maritime Trade Operations agency then said the ship was hit a second time, and India's ports minister later said the crew had abandoned the vessel and one Indian sailor was missing.
 
Another Panama-flagged tanker, On Phoenix, was hit by an unidentified projectile off Oman's coast on Monday. On Tuesday, Iran's armed forces said three oil tankers had struck naval mines while crossing the strait, while the US military says it has cleared the main shipping routes of mines, [according to Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/11/17/kuwait-reports-drone-attacks-as-iran-us-trade-attacks-on-qeshm-and-bahrain).
 
Iran has restricted traffic through the Strait of Hormuz, which normally carries about a fifth of the world's oil, since the war began on 28 February. The US has responded with a naval blockade of Iranian ports and has escorted tankers.
