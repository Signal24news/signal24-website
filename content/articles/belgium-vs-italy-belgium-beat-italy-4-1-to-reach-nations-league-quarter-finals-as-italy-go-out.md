In the Belgium vs Italy match in Brussels on Sunday, Italy needed only a draw to reach the Nations League quarter-finals but lost 4-1 and were knocked out, [Football Italia reported](https://football-italia.net/mancini-italy-will-learn-belgium-better/).
 
Belgium came from behind at the King Baudouin Stadium to join France in the last eight, [according to UEFA](https://www.uefa.com/uefanationsleague/news/02ab-22146db70e40-12d581ab82a9-1000--uefa-nations-league-matchday-6-highlights-belgium-france/).
 
## How the match unfolded
 
Francesco Pio Esposito gave Italy the lead in the 11th minute, [ESPN reported](https://www.espn.com/soccer/report/_/gameId/401861176). Maxim De Cuyper equalised four minutes later, Nathan Ngoy put Belgium ahead soon after, and Jérémy Doku made it 3-1 after 26 minutes, which was the half-time score.
 
After the break, Diego Moreira scored on the counter-attack in the 80th minute from a Kevin De Bruyne pass. In stoppage time, Belgium goalkeeper Senne Lammens saved a penalty from Esposito, [UEFA said](https://www.uefa.com/uefanationsleague/news/02ab-22146db70e40-12d581ab82a9-1000--uefa-nations-league-matchday-6-highlights-belgium-france/).
 
## Group A1 table
 
The defeat left Italy third in Group A1. They finished on 10 points, behind France on 13 and Belgium on 12, [according to the final standings](https://www.espn.com/soccer/report/_/gameId/401861176).
 
Belgium had also won the earlier meeting 2-0 in Rome on 25 September, which was [Roberto Mancini's first match](https://www.aljazeera.com/sports/2026/9/25/belgium-win-deepens-italy-crisis-as-latest-world-cup-rebuild-begins) of his second spell as Italy coach.
 
Elsewhere in the Nations League on Sunday, England ended Spain's record unbeaten run with [a 3-2 win in Madrid](https://signal24.info/article/spain-vs-england-10-man-england-win-3-2-in-madrid-to-end-spains-43-game-unbeaten-run).
 
## What it means for Mancini
 
After the match, Mancini told RAI Sport that Belgium ["were better on the night"](https://football-italia.net/mancini-italy-will-learn-belgium-better/). He said his players felt some fatigue from the France game and struggled to press when the ball reached the wings, but added that "this night will help us going forward".
 
Italy will not play in the Nations League quarter-finals in March and will [start their Euro 2028 qualifying campaign](https://sports.yahoo.com/articles/belgium-defeat-changes-italy-qualifying-111900611.html) then instead. Italy missed out on the 2026 World Cup and had taken [10 points from their last five matches](https://football-italia.net/three-winners-three-losers-latest-italy-squad/) before Sunday's defeat.
