The Strait of Hormuz, the route for about [one-fifth of the world's crude oil](https://www.reuters.com/business/energy/oil-prices-drop-more-than-1-hopes-iran-ukraine-peace-talks-pressure-markets-2026-11-12/), remains closed after the escalation of the conflict between the US and Iran. Months into the conflict, there is no sign that it will reopen soon. Iran says it will stay shut [until its demands are met](https://signal24.info/article/iran-says-strait-of-hormuz-will-stay-shut-until-us-meets-its-demands).
 
## Attacks on ships are rising
 
Attacks on shipping in the region have escalated. Tankers have been set ablaze, drones have hit buildings and oil spills have washed ashore. [Iran's military said](https://www.reuters.com/business/energy/oil-prices-drop-more-than-1-hopes-iran-ukraine-peace-talks-pressure-markets-2026-11-12/) tankers were hitting mines in the strait despite warnings.
 
There were around 37 attacks on ships in October and 10 ships were hit in the first days of November, the highest since the conflict began, according to the Washington Institute for Near East Policy, [cited by Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/business/energy/tankers-continue-burn-middle-east-despite-hopes-de-escalation-2026-11-10/).
 
The United Kingdom Maritime Trade Operations agency also reported an incident involving an oil tanker in the strait, and Iran said it had attacked three tankers for using "unauthorised routes", Reuters reported.
 
Yemen's Houthis have also attacked a pumping station on Saudi Arabia's East-West oil pipeline and other Aramco infrastructure, [according to Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/business/energy/oil-prices-drop-more-than-1-hopes-iran-ukraine-peace-talks-pressure-markets-2026-11-12/).
 
## What it means for oil prices
 
Oil rose by [about $3 a barrel](https://www.reuters.com/business/energy/oil-prices-drop-more-than-1-hopes-iran-ukraine-peace-talks-pressure-markets-2026-11-12/) on Thursday as the disruption continued.
 
Brent crude stood at $95.82 a barrel and US West Texas Intermediate at $88.74, according to the [US Energy Information Administration](https://www.eia.gov/petroleum/supply/weekly/) (EIA).
 
The EIA's weekly report shows US crude inventories rose by 4.7 million barrels to 414.4 million in the first week of November. That offers short-term relief, but stocks are still about 4 percent below the five-year average.
 
With the strait closed, the EIA [expects Brent crude](https://www.eia.gov/outlooks/steo/) to average around $100 a barrel over the next month.
 
## Shipping and the environment
 
The closure is also disrupting trade and damaging the environment. Oil has been seen washing up on the shores of Oman, [Reuters reported](https://www.reuters.com/business/energy/tankers-continue-burn-middle-east-despite-hopes-de-escalation-2026-11-10/).
