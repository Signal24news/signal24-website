KFC closing stores across 10 US states are linked to the financial troubles of franchise operator Tasty Chick'n, which filed for Chapter 11 bankruptcy on Monday, 16 November, after falling sales and rising costs hit its business. Dozens of its restaurants have already shut or are winding down, according to [court filings reported by Fast Company](https://www.fastcompany.com/91644668/kfc-closing-stores-2026-list-doomed-locations-franchise-bankruptcy).
 
The filing does not mean KFC itself is going out of business. It concerns a separate franchise operator that entered bankruptcy with 75 KFC restaurants and nine combined KFC/Taco Bell locations. Tasty Chick'n is seeking to preserve its franchise agreements and sell its restaurant business, [Restaurant Dive reported](https://www.restaurantdive.com/news/kfc-tasty-chicken-yum-brands-bankruptcy-deferred-payments/875030/).
 
## Which states are affected by the KFC closures?
 
Restaurants identified as closed or closing [are spread across](https://www.fastcompany.com/91644668/kfc-closing-stores-2026-list-doomed-locations-franchise-bankruptcy) Alabama, Arkansas, Florida, Illinois, Indiana, Kentucky, Mississippi, Missouri, Tennessee and Virginia. Many of them shut before the bankruptcy petition, so the list is not an announcement of entirely new closures.
 
Tennessee accounts for the largest number of restaurants that had already closed, with 19 locations, according to [Newsweek's state-by-state breakdown](https://www.newsweek.com/kfc-stores-closing-after-franchisee-bankruptcy-list-of-locations-impacted-12735694). Florida follows with 10 and Arkansas with nine. Kentucky and Virginia each have seven, Alabama and Illinois each have four, Mississippi and Missouri each have three, and Indiana has one.
 
Separately, [court documents](https://www.newsweek.com/kfc-stores-closing-after-franchisee-bankruptcy-list-of-locations-impacted-12735694) show nine locations whose leases Tasty Chick'n wants to exit: three in Memphis, Tennessee; three in Cave City, Madisonville and Glasgow, Kentucky; two in Hope and Osceola, Arkansas; and one in Roanoke, Virginia.
 
## Why did Tasty Chick'n file for bankruptcy?
 
Falling sales were central to the franchisee's problems. Restaurant sales declined by more than 15% since fiscal 2024, while restaurant-level profit margins fell from 11.8% in fiscal 2023 to 4% in 2026, according to a [court declaration by president and CEO Thomas Scott](https://www.restaurantdive.com/news/kfc-tasty-chicken-yum-brands-bankruptcy-deferred-payments/875030/). Labour, rent and other operating costs kept rising as sales weakened.
 
The company also owed lenders [upwards of $67 million](https://www.restaurantdive.com/news/kfc-tasty-chicken-yum-brands-bankruptcy-deferred-payments/875030/) after financing restaurant acquisitions, and faces lawsuits from landlords over lease defaults.
 
Its relationship with KFC's owner, Yum Brands, was also under strain. Tasty Chick'n began deferring royalty payments in mid-2025, built up more than $15 million in deferred royalties, and Yum said in September that it intended to terminate the franchise agreements, [Restaurant Dive reported](https://www.restaurantdive.com/news/kfc-tasty-chicken-yum-brands-bankruptcy-deferred-payments/875030/).
 
## Is KFC itself closing?
 
No. The bankruptcy involves Tasty Chick'n and its affiliates, not KFC or Yum Brands, and does not mean all 84 of its restaurants will close. The franchisee wants to [sell its restaurants](https://www.restaurantdive.com/news/kfc-tasty-chicken-yum-brands-bankruptcy-deferred-payments/875030/) "either as an entire portfolio, by individual market, or in combinations of different markets." Some locations remain open during the sale process, while [others have closed or are winding down](https://www.newsweek.com/kfc-stores-closing-after-franchisee-bankruptcy-list-of-locations-impacted-12735694).
 
The closures follow other disruptions for US consumers this week, including a [bank outage at Chase, Bank of America and Wells Fargo](https://signal24.info/article/bank-outage-chase-bank-of-america-and-wells-fargo-customers-hit-by-nationwide-disruption) and an [Apple outage that hit iCloud and Apple Pay](https://signal24.info/article/apple-outage-icloud-apple-pay-and-app-store-disrupted-as-apple-blames-internal-update).
