Robert Pattinson is the new James Bond. Amazon MGM Studios announced on Monday that the star of The Batman and Twilight will play 007 in its first Bond film, [according to the studio's announcement](https://www.aboutamazon.com/news/entertainment/james-bond-robert-pattinson-cast-amazon-mgm-film) and [TheWrap](https://www.thewrap.com/creative-content/movies/robert-pattinson-james-bond-actor/).
 
The film will be directed by Denis Villeneuve, the director of Dune, from a script by Steven Knight, the creator of Peaky Blinders. Amy Pascal and David Heyman are producing, with Tanya Lapointe as executive producer, [Amazon said](https://www.aboutamazon.com/news/entertainment/james-bond-robert-pattinson-cast-amazon-mgm-film).
 
## "The honour of a lifetime"
 
"James Bond is one of the most legendary characters in cinema, and to be entrusted with 007 is the honour of a lifetime," Pattinson said in a statement. He added that calling the role a dream come true would be "an understatement".
 
"I am overjoyed to welcome Robert Pattinson as our new James Bond," Villeneuve said, while Mike Hopkins, head of Prime Video and Amazon MGM Studios, called it "a defining moment for our studio".
 
The official Bond accounts released the first image of Pattinson as 007 and a short video of him in a tuxedo holding a gun, ending with the words "James Bond will return", [Men's Journal reported](https://www.mensjournal.com/news/robert-pattinson-new-james-bond-official).
 
## The seventh James Bond
 
Pattinson, who was born in London and is 40, follows Sean Connery, George Lazenby, Roger Moore, Timothy Dalton, Pierce Brosnan and Daniel Craig in the role, [according to Men's Journal](https://www.mensjournal.com/news/robert-pattinson-new-james-bond-official). Craig's final Bond film, No Time to Die, came out in 2021.
 
He won the part after a long search. Callum Turner, Josh O'Connor and Jack Lowden screen tested in late October, and Pattinson tested during a break from filming The Batman: Part II, [TheWrap reported](https://www.thewrap.com/creative-content/movies/robert-pattinson-james-bond-actor/). [Hypebeast](https://hypebeast.com/?post=6812983), citing The Hollywood Reporter, had named the same shortlist.
 
## Amazon's first Bond film
 
This will be the first Bond film made under Amazon MGM. In February 2025, Amazon MGM formed a joint venture with Barbara Broccoli and Michael G. Wilson of Eon Productions that gave the studio creative control of the franchise, while Broccoli and Wilson remain co-owners, [according to Men's Journal](https://www.mensjournal.com/news/robert-pattinson-new-james-bond-official) and [TheWrap](https://www.thewrap.com/creative-content/movies/robert-pattinson-james-bond-actor/).
 
Amazon has not yet announced a title, filming dates or a release date for the film.
