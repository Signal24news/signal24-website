Israel attack Iran fears grew on Thursday, 26 November, after Israel's military spokesman Effie Defrin told public broadcaster Kan that Israel is "ready to strike with force at any moment, including on our own, without the Americans," [Gulf News reported](https://gulfnews.com/world/mena/israel-ready-to-strike-iran-alone-but-us-has-a-message-for-netanyahu-end-the-wartime-mindset-1.500761767). [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/liveblog/2026/11/26/iran-war-live-explosion-rocks-sanaa-missiles-intercepted-over-riyadh) and [Iran International](https://www.iranintl.com/en/liveblog/202611214578) also reported his comments.
 
Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu was due to convene his security cabinet on Thursday evening, amid reports in The Times of Israel of preparations for another offensive. [No decision to attack](https://gulfnews.com/world/mena/israel-ready-to-strike-iran-alone-but-us-has-a-message-for-netanyahu-end-the-wartime-mindset-1.500761767) has been announced.
 
## Iran: Israel wants to drag the US into war
 
Iran's Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchi accused Israel of trying to pull the United States into another war by claiming Iran is making chemical weapons. "The objective is audacious: to deceive the US into fighting yet another war on its behalf," he wrote on X on Wednesday, [according to Arab News](https://www.arabnews.com/middle-east/iran-says-israel-trying-to-deceive-us-into-war-with-chemical-weapons-claim-3014026).
 
He was responding to Israeli Defence Minister Israel Katz, who said on Tuesday that Iran "is in the process of developing chemical weapons but has not yet reached the capability to attack Israel with them," [Arab News reported](https://www.arabnews.com/middle-east/iran-says-israel-trying-to-deceive-us-into-war-with-chemical-weapons-claim-3014026). Iran has dismissed the claims as propaganda.
 
## Divisions in Israel
 
Not everyone in Israel's security establishment sees a need to attack now. Military chief Eyal Zamir and some Mossad researchers reportedly see [no immediate need for another offensive](https://gulfnews.com/world/mena/israel-ready-to-strike-iran-alone-but-us-has-a-message-for-netanyahu-end-the-wartime-mindset-1.500761767), Gulf News said.
 
Netanyahu lost his majority in the 27 October election and is caretaker prime minister while Gadi Eisenkot tries to form a government. His opponents accuse him of seeking renewed conflict to stay in power, while Netanyahu accuses them of playing down security threats, [according to Gulf News](https://gulfnews.com/world/mena/israel-ready-to-strike-iran-alone-but-us-has-a-message-for-netanyahu-end-the-wartime-mindset-1.500761767). He has reportedly pressed the Trump administration to back another offensive and sent Mossad chief Roman Gofman to Washington this month.
 
## What the US is saying
 
US special envoy Jared Kushner said in a podcast interview with German businessman Mathias Dopfner on Wednesday that Israel "needs to get out of the constant wartime mindset and focus on economic diplomacy," [Gulf News reported](https://gulfnews.com/world/mena/israel-ready-to-strike-iran-alone-but-us-has-a-message-for-netanyahu-end-the-wartime-mindset-1.500761767). Asked about new US strikes, he said President Donald Trump "always keeps all his options on the table." Kushner also said he had met Iranian representatives several times, without saying when.
 
Trump told the New York Post that the US can keep funding military operations against Iran until [about the end of January](https://abc45.com/news/nation-world/trump-says-iran-war-funded-through-january-as-israel-warns-of-chemical-weapons-development-strait-hormuz). He also said toppling Iran's government is not a US goal, a key difference with Israel. Democrats, who are set to take control of both houses of Congress, are pushing to limit money for offensive operations against Iran before the 11 December funding deadline.
 
## More tankers hit in Hormuz
 
Iran's Revolutionary Guards said two oil tankers hit sea mines and caught fire near Oman's Khasab port on Thursday, while crossing the Strait of Hormuz without Iran's permission, [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/liveblog/2026/11/26/iran-war-live-explosion-rocks-sanaa-missiles-intercepted-over-riyadh) and [ABC News](https://abcnews.com/International/live-updates/iran-live-updates-tehran-strait-hormuz-safe-demands/?id=138369648) reported. The claim has not been independently verified.
