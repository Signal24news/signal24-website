Bruno Mars' father, Peter Hernandez, has died, the singer announced on Thursday, 19 November, in an Instagram tribute to the man he credits with teaching him everything he knows about music, [Yahoo News reported](https://uk.news.yahoo.com/bruno-mars-announces-dad-peter-070000590.html). Several outlets, citing Just Jared, reported that Hernandez was 74. No cause of death has been given.
 
"Last week I got to hug my father one last time," Mars wrote, according to [IBTimes UK](https://www.ibtimes.co.uk/bruno-mars-father-peter-hernandez-dies-1833496). "In my eyes he was the greatest dad in the world, and I will miss him everyday until we meet again," he added, [AllHipHop reported](https://allhiphop.com/news/bruno-mars-father-peter-hernandez-dies/).
 
## Who was Peter Hernandez?
 
Hernandez was a Brooklyn-born percussionist and doo-wop bandleader who built his career in Hawaii, [according to Nacionale](https://www.nacionale.com/en/articles/who-was-bruno-mars-dad-peter-hernandez-dies-74-love-notes-waikiki). He grew up in New York, where he went along to his own father's gigs and fell in love with doo-wop, [AllHipHop reported](https://allhiphop.com/news/bruno-mars-father-peter-hernandez-dies/).
 
Mars, whose real name is also Peter Hernandez, joined his father on stage as a small child doing an Elvis Presley impression, and at six played "Little Elvis" in the 1992 film Honeymoon in Vegas, [Nacionale reported](https://www.nacionale.com/en/articles/who-was-bruno-mars-dad-peter-hernandez-dies-74-love-notes-waikiki). His father taught him the bongos, which Mars has credited for his sense of rhythm.
 
"He was a ham," Hernandez said of his young son in a 2014 interview with the Hawaiian magazine MidWeek, [People reported](https://www.yahoo.com/entertainment/celebrity/deaths-obituaries/articles/know-bruno-mars-parents-bernadette-190238756.html).
 
## A second loss for the family
 
Mars' mother, Bernadette, a hula dancer and singer who moved to Hawaii from the Philippines, died in June 2013 at the age of 55, [AP reported at the time](https://www.cbsnews.com/losangeles/news/ap-source-bruno-mars-mother-dies-at-age-55). Mars closed his tribute with a message to both of his parents: "I love you, Pops," [according to AllHipHop](https://allhiphop.com/news/bruno-mars-father-peter-hernandez-dies/).
 
Peter and Bernadette Hernandez raised six children in a musical family, [People reported](https://www.yahoo.com/entertainment/celebrity/deaths-obituaries/articles/know-bruno-mars-parents-bernadette-190238756.html).
