Patrick Stewart's cause of death was cardiopulmonary arrest and a neurocognitive disorder with Lewy bodies, according to his death certificate obtained by TMZ, [People reported](https://people.com/patrick-stewart-cause-of-death-confirmed-12201341). The British actor, best known for Star Trek and the X-Men films, died on 28 October at the age of 86.
 
The certificate, reported on 19 November, matches his family's earlier statement that Stewart had Alzheimer's disease and Lewy body dementia, [E! News reported](https://www.eonline.com/news/1438263/patrick-stewarts-cause-of-death-was-alzheimers-lewy-body-dementia). It also shows his remains underwent alkaline hydrolysis, a form of water cremation, on 3 November, [according to TMZ](https://www.tmz.com/2026/11/19/patrick-stewart-cause-of-death/).
 
## What his family said
 
His longtime spokesperson, Kelly Bush Novak, said in October that Stewart died at his home in Los Angeles in the loving care of his wife, Sunny Ozell, [People reported](https://people.com/patrick-stewart-cause-of-death-confirmed-12201341). The statement described a "brief and poignant journey with Alzheimer's Disease and Lewy Body Dementia," [Far Out Magazine reported](https://faroutmagazine.co.uk/patrick-stewart-official-cause-death-confirmed/).
 
Bush Novak also said Stewart had supported causes including human rights, survivors of domestic violence, refugees, veterans' mental health services and animal welfare, [according to People](https://people.com/patrick-stewart-cause-of-death-confirmed-12201341).
 
On 11 November, two weeks after his death, Ozell wrote on Instagram that her grief "continues to come in waves" and thanked fans for their messages, [People reported](https://people.com/patrick-stewart-cause-of-death-confirmed-12201341). "We left nothing unsaid. He knew I loved him, and I know he loved me," she wrote.
 
His Star Trek co-star William Shatner called him "a friend, a colleague, and a magnificent actor," [Variety reported](https://variety.com/2026/tv/news/william-shatner-star-trek-patrick-stewart-picard-tributes-1236934479/).
 
## How old was Patrick Stewart?
 
Stewart was born on 13 July 1940 in Mirfield, West Yorkshire, England, [according to People](https://people.com/patrick-stewart-cause-of-death-confirmed-12201341), which made him 86 when he died. Some websites have given his age as 88, which is wrong.
 
## A career from Shakespeare to Star Trek
 
Stewart began as a stage actor and spent years in Shakespeare productions before becoming known worldwide as Captain Jean-Luc Picard in Star Trek: The Next Generation from 1987. He played Picard across the show's seven seasons and four films, and returned to the role in Star Trek: Picard in 2020, [Variety reported](https://variety.com/2026/tv/news/william-shatner-star-trek-patrick-stewart-picard-tributes-1236934479/). He was also known to a new generation as Professor Charles Xavier in the X-Men films.
