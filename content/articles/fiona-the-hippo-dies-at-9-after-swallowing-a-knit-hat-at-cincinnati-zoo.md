Fiona the hippo, the Cincinnati Zoo's celebrated Nile hippopotamus, died on Thursday, 19 November, at the age of 9, about 11 days after a knit hat landed in her habitat and she swallowed it, [PBS NewsHour reported](https://www.pbs.org/newshour/nation/cincinnati-zoo-says-fiona-the-hippo-has-died-more-than-a-week-after-ingesting-a-hat). She would have turned 10 in January.
 
The zoo [announced her death](https://cincinnatizoo.org/beloved-hippo-fiona-has-passed-away/) on Thursday, saying its team made multiple attempts to move the hat out of her system but that it was "ultimately too much to overcome." "She fought until she couldn't anymore," said zoo director Dave Jenike.
 
## What happened to Fiona the hippo?
 
A visitor tossed the hat into the air in front of the enclosure, where a ceiling fan caught it and flung it through a small opening above the viewing windows into Fiona's habitat, [according to PBS NewsHour](https://www.pbs.org/newshour/nation/cincinnati-zoo-says-fiona-the-hippo-has-died-more-than-a-week-after-ingesting-a-hat). The zoo said the hat [ended up in her habitat by accident](https://cincinnatizoo.org/beloved-hippo-fiona-has-passed-away/).
 
Vets sedated her and carried out a colonoscopy with a nine-foot scope, but could not find the hat. Specialists told the zoo that surgery is not an option for a full-grown hippo, [US News reported](https://www.usnews.com/news/us/articles/2026-11-18/cincinnati-zoo-says-fiona-the-hippo-cant-undergo-surgery-after-swallowing-a-hat). Instead the team gave her fluids and medication to help her pass it naturally, and on Wednesday had her stand on a vibration plate borrowed from a local horse farm to help move it through her digestive tract, [Smithsonian Magazine reported](https://www.smithsonianmag.com/smart-news/a-child-tossed-a-hat-and-a-ceiling-fan-sent-it-flying-into-a-hippos-zoo-enclosure-where-the-animal-ate-it-heres-how-the-care-team-is-responding-180989894/).
 
The zoo said the supportive care its team gave her on Thursday [did not work and she did not recover](https://cincinnatizoo.org/beloved-hippo-fiona-has-passed-away/).
 
## Why the world knew Fiona
 
Fiona was born six weeks early in January 2017, weighing 29 pounds, far less than a healthy newborn hippo, [The Washington Post reported](https://www.washingtonpost.com/nation/2026/11/19/cincinnati-zoos-beloved-hippo-fiona-dead-9-years-old/). The zoo shared her progress online, and her first birthday was streamed to thousands of fans on Facebook Live. She went on to inspire [books, beer, clothing and an ice cream flavour](https://www.foxnews.com/us/beloved-hippo-fiona-dies-cincinnati-zoo-swallowing-visitors-hat).
 
Before her death was announced, local students were [making get-well cards for her](https://www.washingtonpost.com/nation/2026/11/19/cincinnati-zoos-beloved-hippo-fiona-dead-9-years-old/). Hippos typically live 35 to 50 years, according to the Post.
 
Tributes followed. The Cincinnati Bengals called her ["The queen of the Queen City"](https://x.com/Bengals/status/2123524040744923481), and US Senator Bernie Moreno of Ohio called her ["Ohio's fighter"](https://x.com/berniemoreno/status/2123534159393997163).
