Jensen Huang has been reinstated as Nvidia CEO, president and board member, effective immediately, the company [announced on Monday, 23 November](https://nvidianews.nvidia.com/news/nvidia-board-reinstates-jensen-huang-as-president-ceo-and-board-director). His return comes 12 days after the board [accepted his resignation](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/articles/nvidia-stock-soars-27-bell-141538654.html).
 
Nvidia said the board [voted unanimously](https://nvidianews.nvidia.com/news/nvidia-board-reinstates-jensen-huang-as-president-ceo-and-board-director) after a series of meetings with Huang, and that it was satisfied with his candor and his ability to lead the company.
 
## Why the board changed its mind
 
Huang [resigned on 12 November](https://signal24.info/article/jensen-huang-resigns-as-nvidia-ceo-colette-kress-named-interim-chief) after reports about his personal relationships, and the board opened an independent investigation. In Monday's statement, Nvidia said that investigation began after [reporting that Huang was the target of a foreign intelligence effort](https://nvidianews.nvidia.com/news/nvidia-board-reinstates-jensen-huang-as-president-ceo-and-board-director).
 
The board said it had [received input from senior levels of the United States government](https://nvidianews.nvidia.com/news/nvidia-board-reinstates-jensen-huang-as-president-ceo-and-board-director) and, based on that input, was confident his return poses no national security risk. [NBC News](https://www.nbcnews.com/politics/trump-administration/live-blog/trump-justice-department-white-house-tree-congress-2026-live-updates-rcna610817) also reported the government input line.
 
Huang said he takes the role back "with the greatest humility and renewed commitment," according to [Nvidia](https://nvidianews.nvidia.com/news/nvidia-board-reinstates-jensen-huang-as-president-ceo-and-board-director) and [NPR](https://www.npr.org/2026/11/23/nx-s1-6050910/nvidias-jensen-huang-trump).
 
## What Trump said
 
President Donald Trump backed the decision. "The NVIDIA Board of Directors has made the correct and proper decision to return Jensen Huang to the position of CEO," he wrote on Truth Social, [as quoted by Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/article/nvidia-stock-jumps-as-dc-makes-stunning-u-turn-on-jensen-huang-113419437.html).
 
Trump also told [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/23/trump-nvidia-jensen-huang.html) that he had spoken with Nvidia's board "directly and indirectly" before the decision, and that he spoke with Huang on Sunday night.
 
## Nvidia stock soars
 
Nvidia stock rose [more than 30% on Monday](https://www.cnn.com/2026/11/23/markets/us-stocks-rebound-intl), according to CNN and [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/23/anthropic-nvidia-shares.html). Yahoo Finance reported that the shares were up [as much as 36% during the day](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/articles/nvidia-stock-soars-huang-reinstatement-171426068.html), the biggest intraday jump since 2000, after his exit had sent them down by nearly 28%.
 
The rally lifted the wider market. The Nasdaq Composite was up [more than 7% in mid-morning trading](https://www.wpsdlocal6.com/news/nvidia-shares-soar-more-than-30-after-huang-reinstated-as-ceo/article_4ccaafbb-cba6-4744-af17-8ac5a6e8a4a1.html), CNN reported, after a [heavy tech sell-off last week](https://signal24.info/article/stock-market-down-today-nasdaq-sinks-more-than-3-as-the-ai-sell-off-deepens).
 
## What happens to Colette Kress
 
[Colette Kress](https://signal24.info/article/who-is-colette-kress-nvidias-interim-ceo-after-jensen-huangs-resignation), who served as interim CEO, will stay on as chief financial officer, and Harvey C. Jones remains chair of the board, [Nvidia said](https://nvidianews.nvidia.com/news/nvidia-board-reinstates-jensen-huang-as-president-ceo-and-board-director). The board thanked Kress for her "outstanding service as interim CEO."
 
## The investigation is not over
 
Nvidia said the independent investigation [will continue to its conclusion](https://nvidianews.nvidia.com/news/nvidia-board-reinstates-jensen-huang-as-president-ceo-and-board-director) and that Huang supports it. The board has also set up a new committee to oversee trade compliance and national security risks and is hiring a new chief compliance officer.
 
Congress is still looking at the case. Three House committees will hold a [closed-door interview with Huang on 1 December](https://www.nbcnews.com/politics/trump-administration/live-blog/trump-justice-department-white-house-tree-congress-2026-live-updates-rcna610817), and a spokesperson for the House Intelligence Committee said his reinstatement "changes nothing" for that process. Huang will also testify at an [open Senate Banking Committee hearing on 3 December](https://www.cnn.com/2026/11/23/business/live-news/jensen-huang-reinstated-as-ceo-of-nvidia), according to the committee.
