The Trump Xi Putin summit in Shenzhen ended on Thursday, 19 November, with the leaders of the US, China and Russia agreeing that a "nuclear war cannot be won and must never be fought," [France 24 reported](https://www.france24.com/en/asia-pacific/20261119-xi-trump-putin-talks-live-china-us-russia-leaders-hold-landmark-three-way-meeting), quoting the statement published by Chinese state media. It was the first official meeting of the three nuclear-armed powers, [ABC News (Australia) said](https://www.abc.net.au/news/2026-11-19/trump-xi-putin-meeting-live-blog-apec-summit/107394202).
 
The talks took place at Kylin Villa on the sidelines of the Asia-Pacific Economic Cooperation (APEC) leaders' meeting, which China is hosting on 18 and 19 November. They lasted more than an hour and started about two hours late, [Fox News reported](https://www.foxnews.com/politics/trumps-historic-summit-xi-putin-marks-diplomatic-milestone-breakthroughs-remain-elusive). Before the three-way meeting, Trump met Putin for about 40 minutes and Xi for more than an hour.
 
## What the three leaders agreed
 
The leaders issued a joint statement with four points, [according to China's Xinhua news agency as reported by Seoul Economic Daily](https://en.sedaily.com/world/2026/11/19/us-china-russia-hold-first-trilateral-summit-declare-no):
 
- **Nuclear weapons:** a nuclear war cannot be won, and the three countries will hold talks on reducing nuclear risks and stopping the spread of nuclear weapons.
- **Energy:** the three countries will expand the production and supply of refined petroleum products and stressed stable energy flows.
- **International courts:** they said states only take on treaty obligations voluntarily, respected the immunity of state officials, and said the International Criminal Court and similar bodies should not abuse their jurisdiction.
- **UN reform:** as permanent members of the UN Security Council, they will work to reform the United Nations.
 
The statement set no targets for cutting nuclear arsenals. The US, China and Russia hold the world's three largest nuclear arsenals, [CBS News noted](https://www.cbsnews.com/news/trump-xi-putin-china-apec-summit/).
 
## What Trump, Xi and Putin said
 
"We are really making history," Trump said, according to [Fox News](https://www.foxnews.com/politics/trumps-historic-summit-xi-putin-marks-diplomatic-milestone-breakthroughs-remain-elusive). On nuclear cuts, he said: "It's a very hard thing to do, because once you have the nuclear weapons, it's very hard to not have them."
 
Xi called the meeting a "groundbreaking move" and said: "Our positive interaction is not optional, but a must," [Fox News reported](https://www.foxnews.com/politics/trumps-historic-summit-xi-putin-marks-diplomatic-milestone-breakthroughs-remain-elusive). Chinese state media quoted him as calling the talks "positive, constructive, fruitful," [according to France 24](https://www.france24.com/en/asia-pacific/20261119-xi-trump-putin-talks-live-china-us-russia-leaders-hold-landmark-three-way-meeting).
 
Putin told reporters afterwards: "The most important thing, and I have no doubt about this, is that President Trump genuinely wants to resolve conflicts, including the one in Ukraine," [TASS reported](https://tass.com/politics/2221563). He also said Ukraine and Europe were not yet ready to compromise and that he would like to continue three-way meetings, [according to RIA as cited by Newsquawk](https://www.newsquawk.com/headlines/russian-president-putin-says-he-would-like-to-continue-trilateral-meetings-with-the-leaders-of-china-and-the-us-ria-reports).
 
## Ukraine and Iran
 
No breakthrough on Ukraine was announced, and the joint statement does not mention the war. Trump said he hoped both sides wanted "an end to that war," [Fox News reported](https://www.foxnews.com/politics/trumps-historic-summit-xi-putin-marks-diplomatic-milestone-breakthroughs-remain-elusive). The White House had earlier said Ukraine would not be discussed because no Ukrainian officials would attend, but Trump later said he expected it to come up, [CBS News reported](https://www.cbsnews.com/news/trump-xi-putin-china-apec-summit/).
 
On Iran, Trump said: "Both the United States and China have an interest in ensuring that energy flows through the Strait of Hormuz," according to [Fox News](https://www.foxnews.com/politics/trumps-historic-summit-xi-putin-marks-diplomatic-milestone-breakthroughs-remain-elusive). Trump had said earlier this month he would [raise the Iran war with Xi and Putin](https://signal24.info/article/trump-says-iran-land-blockade-is-in-full-force-plans-to-raise-the-war-with-xi-and-putin). ## A reporter barred from the talks
 
A Bloomberg reporter was barred from the three-way talks after asking Trump whether former Nvidia chief Jensen Huang had been "disloyal to the United States," [Fox News reported](https://www.foxnews.com/politics/trumps-historic-summit-xi-putin-marks-diplomatic-milestone-breakthroughs-remain-elusive), citing a White House pool report that said Chinese officials blocked her access. Huang [resigned as Nvidia CEO last week](https://signal24.info/article/jensen-huang-resigns-as-nvidia-ceo-colette-kress-named-interim-chief).
