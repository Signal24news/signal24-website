Real Madrid vs PSV finished 5-1 at the Santiago Bernabeu on Tuesday night, as Jose Mourinho's side [came from a goal down](https://www.uefa.com/uefachampionsleague/news/02ab-222684fc8e44-2b1a32c90a7a-1000--champions-league-highlights-and-round-up-real-madrid-sta/) to score five times in the second half and make it five wins from five in the Champions League league phase.
 
## The goals
 
PSV led at half-time through [Ivan Perisic's 32nd-minute penalty](https://www.tntsports.co.uk/football/champions-league/2026-2027/real-madrid-psv_mtc21878582/live-commentary.shtml). Madrid's response was immediate. Federico Valverde [equalised in the 50th minute](https://www.espn.com/soccer/report/_/gameId/401915298), and Jude Bellingham, brought on at the break, put the hosts ahead on 60 minutes with a [sensational long-range effort](https://www.uefa.com/uefachampionsleague/news/02ab-222684fc8e44-2b1a32c90a7a-1000--champions-league-highlights-and-round-up-real-madrid-sta/).
 
Kylian Mbappe then scored in the 64th and 84th minutes, both [from inside the box](https://www.uefa.com/uefachampionsleague/news/02ab-222684fc8e44-2b1a32c90a7a-1000--champions-league-highlights-and-round-up-real-madrid-sta/), and was named Player of the Match. Vinicius Junior, who hit the woodwork in each half, completed the scoring in the 87th minute with [his first Champions League goal this season](https://www.espn.com/soccer/story/_/id/50586644/bellingham-mourinho-real-madrid-stunner).
 
## The turning point
 
Madrid were [poor in the first half](https://www.espn.com/soccer/report/_/gameId/401915298). [Vinicius hit the post](https://sports.yahoo.com/soccer/articles/immediate-reaction-real-madrid-5-215358764.html) early on, but they could not find a rhythm, and PSV went in ahead. Mourinho responded at half-time with [a triple substitution](https://www.espn.com/soccer/story/_/id/50586644/bellingham-mourinho-real-madrid-stunner) that included Bellingham, and Valverde's equaliser came within minutes of the restart.
 
## What the coaches said
 
"The second half was a question of mentality," Mourinho told Movistar, [ESPN reported](https://www.espn.com/soccer/story/_/id/50586644/bellingham-mourinho-real-madrid-stunner). "We made changes, and Jude is a different player." Goalkeeper Thibaut Courtois said the clips Mourinho showed at half-time changed the team's attitude.
 
PSV coach Peter Bosz said his side [played with courage early on and had four chances but scored only one](https://www.uefa.com/uefachampionsleague/match/2049625--real-madrid-vs-psv/), and admitted they did not do enough after the interval.
 
## What it means for the league phase
 
Madrid top the table with [15 points and a goal difference of +10](https://www.uefa.com/uefachampionsleague/match/2049625--real-madrid-vs-psv/), having now beaten [Inter Milan, Roma, RB Leipzig, AEK Athens](https://www.espn.com/soccer/story/_/id/50586644/bellingham-mourinho-real-madrid-stunner) and PSV. Their next European game is [away to Arsenal on 9 December](https://www.espn.com/soccer/story/_/id/50586644/bellingham-mourinho-real-madrid-stunner). The [top eight at the end of the league phase go straight into the last 16](https://www.uefa.com/uefachampionsleague/news/02a8-216eb10b789e-0768c05cf31b-1000--champions-league-europa-league-and-conference-league-leag/).
 
After Tuesday's games PSV sit [28th with four points](https://www.uefa.com/uefachampionsleague/match/2049625--real-madrid-vs-psv/) from [one win, one draw and three defeats](https://www.espn.com/soccer/report/_/gameId/401915298). That is outside the top 24 who reach the knockout stage, with three games left to climb back.
